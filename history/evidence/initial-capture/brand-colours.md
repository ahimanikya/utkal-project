# Utkal Project · approved brand colours

The Founder approved all three colour families and renamed the third **Sukha Ghasi**. The former name **Poda Ghasi** is retained only in historical discussion or earlier evidence.

| Brand colour | Working value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Kankada Pathara | `#91462F` | Earthy identity accents |
| Nila Sagara | `#1D4658` | Main wordmark, links and deep backgrounds |
| Sukha Ghasi · muted olive | `#74633B` | Supporting emphasis |
| Sukha Ghasi · dry straw | `#C1A263` | Decoration and highlights |

These are **three brand colour families**; olive and straw are two tones of Sukha Ghasi. Warm limewash `#F2E6D0` and earth ink `#302E28` are supporting neutrals.

The Founder connected Sukha Ghasi with cow dung and paddy straw used for cooking in Odisha. Preserve that as his stated inspiration. The naming decision does not establish a researched definition of the term or a historical claim for publication.

Approval: “Yes - I will use all three as brand colors”. The earlier clarification was “Dry straw and muted olive”. The digital values carry forward the reviewed comparison and can be calibrated for print later.

Project name and tagline remain **Utkal Project — Rediscover Utkal. Reimagine Odisha.** The final logo is still to be selected. See [structured colour record](BRAND-COLOURS.json).
