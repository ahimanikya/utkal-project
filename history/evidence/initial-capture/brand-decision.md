# Utkal Project brand decision

**Status: approved by Founder & Editor-in-Chief Ahimanikya Satapathy.**

- Project and website name: **Utkal Project**.
- Approved tagline: **Rediscover Utkal. Reimagine Odisha.**
- Approval: the Founder said **“OK lock that”** after the name and tagline were presented together.
- This replaces the preview tagline “Discover Utkal. Connect with Odisha.”
- Use the approved wording consistently in the website header, footer and future project identity materials. Preserve capitalization and the full stop after each sentence.

The canonical preview values are in `src/data/site.json`. This records a brand choice; no audience testing or measured virality is claimed. Historical records and earlier review evidence retain their original wording.

## Brand colours approved

Kankada Pathara, Nila Sagara and **Sukha Ghasi** are the three approved brand colour families. Sukha Ghasi replaces the earlier Poda Ghasi label and includes dry-straw and muted-olive tones. See [colour decision](../../brand/BRAND-COLOURS.md). The final logo is still pending selection.
