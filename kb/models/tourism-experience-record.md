# Tourism experience record template

Use this template to extend an existing cultural or place entry into a practical experience candidate for the [pilot](../specs/tourism-experience-pilot.md). Retain the existing entity ID and sources. A new record describes an activity and its delivery; it does not replace the place, person or craft entry.

## Identity and story

- Experience ID and linked KB entities:
- English and Odia names, with language review status:
- Place, district and meeting location:
- Short visitor promise and why this matters locally:
- Story, sourced facts, local testimony and beliefs clearly distinguished:
- Images, creator, rights and consent reference:

## Visit and local benefit

- Proposed activity and duration; who it suits:
- Host or operating organisation; confirmation state:
- What the host gains; proposed payment versus actual paid amount:
- Public booking/contact route approved for publication:
- Opening/availability, season, weather alternative and closure exceptions:
- Minimum/maximum group size and host-confirmed capacity:
- Transport, approach, walk distance and rest opportunities:
- Mobility access, toilets, water, language support and food/allergen questions:
- Price, unit, inclusions/exclusions, quote date, validity and cancellation terms:
- Cultural etiquette, photography and venue permissions:
- Alternatives if the host, venue or weather makes the visit unsuitable:

## Evidence and review

- Stage: desk lead / locally confirmed / trial assessed:
- Publication state: internal draft / awaiting review / approved / listed / withdrawn:
- Evidence for each variable operating claim, source ID and locator:
- Confirmed by and role; checked date; next review date:
- Permission scope and restricted-record reference, without private documents or contact details:
- Unknowns, conflicts and changes since the last check:
- Human owner, reviewer and decision reference:
- Trial date and observations, or explicitly “not trialled”:
- Next action, dependency and canonical work ID:

Use null or “unknown” for missing values; do not substitute zero or a positive assumption. Record the source's exact date and currency. An accessibility answer must describe the actual condition, not use an unsupported “accessible” badge. Use only approved public contacts in Git; hold sensitive intake until an approved restricted home exists. Suggested recheck interval is 90 days, with reconfirmation before a scheduled group visit.

## Readiness checklist

A locally confirmed record needs a willing host or responsible operating body, dated operating answers, stated restrictions and permission for the public information. A trial needs separate actual arrangements. Public listing additionally needs editorial review and publication authorization. Keep outstanding limitations visible in all three cases.
