## What this first edition records

The initial seventeen retrospectively captured entries cover the founding intent, opportunity mission, project family, human-led governance, first setup, website name, tagline alternatives, logo explorations, Odia lettering, the Founder’s material colour names, Sukha Ghasi and the reported discussions with Pradip Biswal. The capture date is known. Exact dates of the earlier conversations and Pradip Biswal discussions are not supplied. Do not replace missing event dates with the date this record was written.

## Working habit from now on

For material changes, add a ledger entry as part of completing the work: naming, branding, purpose, requirements, governance, new collaborators, releases, major experiments and lessons. Avoid logging every minor layout fix or routine command.

1. Record what changed and why, who contributed the idea, and who decided.
2. Preserve the actual user wording when a quotation matters. Label paraphrases and AI recommendations. Never reconstruct another person’s words or memories.
3. Distinguish proposals, human approvals, implementation and public release. A conversation or interest in helping does not appoint someone to a role.
4. Attach a stable decision ID, source path or commit when available. Preserve milestone assets and their provenance in versioned evidence folders.
5. Append corrections or superseding decisions as new IDs linked to the earlier entry. Do not silently rewrite history. For a necessary redaction, retain a minimal dated notice explaining the change without repeating removed information.
6. Update the readable ledger and story essays from the records. Label narrative interpretation and link to supporting entry IDs.
7. Keep sensitive contact details, private correspondence and consent material out of general history exports. Capture only what the task authorizes; these current records contain no contact details.

The Founder remains the final human editor. The assistant maintains the ledger when carrying out relevant authorized work. The ledger is a historical record, not a replacement for formal approvals or the project’s governance.

## Preservation and handover

The canonical copy for this task is this `history/` directory in the local Utkal Project repository staging area. Plain UTF-8 Markdown and JSONL are intentionally used so the record can be read without a particular service. Images, original generation prompts, decisions and local checksums are bundled for portability.

The complete first edition is saved locally. The register-model application publishes the textual ledger and stories to the private repository. Binary evidence assets remain in the local history archive; consult evidence/AVAILABILITY.md before assuming an image is available in Git. No new backup service or scheduled process is established. At the next repository publication, commit the ledger and its evidence together and record the resulting immutable commit. A local checksum manifest is not protection against loss of the machine.

When significant work resumes, read this ledger along with current decision records, then append to it. Preserve the original asset/decision archives even when a later brand or product version replaces their use.

Five subsequent entries record dominant colour, concept preference, corrected stone name, the story request and the register-model approval. Current work is tracked in [the operating dashboard](../registers/DASHBOARD.md).
