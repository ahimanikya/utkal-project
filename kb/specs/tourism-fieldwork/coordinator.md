# Local tourism research coordinator brief

The coordinator turns six desk leads into honest, usable visit records. The role is vacant and reports to **Ahimanikya Satapathy, Founder & Editor-in-Chief**. This is a proposed short assignment, not a job offer or appointment.

## Assignment and skills

Plan and accompany local research in Bhubaneswar, Cuttack and Puri district. The person should communicate comfortably in Odia and English, arrange visits respectfully, record accurate practical information, compare written costs, and distinguish observations from promises. Experience with crafts, museums, community research or responsible tourism is useful. They must disclose a financial or family relationship with any proposed supplier or host.

A selection conversation should ask for a short writing sample, an example of handling refusal or a changed appointment, availability for local travel, and how they would describe an access barrier without assuming a visitor’s abilities. Use a short hypothetical exercise, not unpaid productive fieldwork. Agree compensation, reimbursable costs and payment timing before appointment.

## Six person-days of work

| Allocation | Work | Return |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Two planning days | Identify appropriate recipients, prepare permissions, obtain quotes after authorization, confirm needs and routing | Six appointment candidates or recorded refusals, cost comparison and a workable daily route |
| Three field days | Accompany the researcher, check actual conditions, respect permissions and reconcile the day’s costs | Dated visit records and a short daily update |
| One synthesis day | Resolve contradictions, prepare public summaries and a barrier log | Six outcomes, which may include deferred/declined visits, and a reconciled expense record |

The six-day allowance comes from the [capacity plan](../tourism-pilot-capacity.md): ₹2,000 per person-day is a planning input, not an agreed rate. Three field days and two stops per day remain routing assumptions. Travel to Odisha, overnight stays and extra days require re-costing. Do not promise that every candidate will be confirmed.

## Decision rights and reporting

The Founder approves appointment, recipients/messages, spending ceiling and publication. The coordinator may propose changes and record observations within the agreed assignment. They cannot promise government affiliation, business referrals, bookings, grants or inclusion in the encyclopedia. No human reports to an AI persona.

Before departure, send the human lead the agreed route and check-in arrangement. After each day, report completed and missed visits, changes in consent, access problems, costs incurred and the next decision needed. If weather, local advice, permission or access makes a visit unsuitable, stop or reschedule that visit and notify the human lead. Do not attempt to meet a numerical target by bypassing a refusal.

## Deliverables and acceptance

Return one [confirmation record](host-confirmation.md) per candidate, with unknowns retained; six short story outlines or explanations of why they could not be prepared; a practical barrier log; and quotes/expenses reconciled to the [cost tracker](cost-tracker.md). Photographs and quotes must carry scoped permissions and original evidence references. Host review checks factual/consent accuracy and does not buy favourable coverage.

Acceptance means the records are complete and honest, not that all six hosts agreed. The Founder reviews the handoff, resolves publication decisions and decides whether to expand the pilot.

## Appointment fields

Full name, accepted scope, availability, agreed fee/inclusions, human supervisor, authorized actions, expense ceiling, agreement date and restricted record home are all **unassigned**. Keep personal contact, identity, payment and signed consent material out of public Git. Choose the restricted home before receiving sensitive documents.
