# Six experience fieldwork pack

Prepare six research visits that can support useful stories and credible visitor information. **This is a handoff draft:** no coordinator is appointed, no outreach has been sent, and no visit or expenditure is booked.

## Start here

- [Coordinator assignment](coordinator.md): vacancy, skills, six-day allocation, deliverables and human reporting.
- [Confirmation checklist](host-confirmation.md): operating facts, access, costs and separate recording/publication permissions.
- [Operator readiness and trial decision pack](operator-readiness.md): turn dated findings into a scoped assessment, resolve unknowns and prepare a human trial decision without implying a booking.
- [Six introduction drafts](outreach-drafts.md): recipient-specific wording for Founder review.
- [Quote and expense tracker](cost-tracker.md) and [editable workbook](cost-tracker.xlsx): replace assumptions with quotes, commitments and payments without double counting.
- [MICE route follow up](mice-follow-up.md): latest retrieval limits and an unsent departmental enquiry.

## Proposed first six visits

| Brief | Area | What the research should establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Kala Bhoomi](kala-bhoomi.md) | Bhubaneswar | Ticket/guide charges; level access, seating and toilets; time away from staff duties; whether maker compensation is separate from museum fees. |
| [Dhauli](dhauli.md) | Bhubaneswar | Steps and slopes, shade and rest, vehicle drop-off, weather exposure, guide fee and current venue rules. No show, event or route duration is assumed. |
| [Cuttack Tarakasi](tarakasi.md) | Cuttack | Demonstration fee versus purchase price; maximum group; tools/heat/workspace restrictions; seating and toilets; language; cancellation and unfinished work. |
| [Odisha State Maritime Museum](maritime-museum.md) | Cuttack | Current admission and guide cost, group limit, gallery closures, approach, steps, seating, toilets and photography. |
| [Raghurajpur](raghurajpur.md) | Puri district | Fee and materials, no-purchase option, group capacity, toilets and steps, transport meeting point, photo permissions and weather fallback. |
| [Pipili appliqué](pipili.md) | Puri district | Making-versus-retail location, fee and materials, group size, tool/production restrictions, language, seating, toilets and separate reproduction rights. |

The order above is a reading order, not a scheduled route. The three-day pairing remains a cost assumption until actual appointments, travel and individual access needs are checked. Use the [22-lead shortlist](../../research/collections/tourism-experience-shortlist.md) for alternatives; substitutions need a recorded reason and Founder agreement.

## Before the first call or visit

The Founder selects the scope, names the coordinator, approves actual recipients/messages and sets the payer and expense ceiling. A secure home for contact/consent/financial records must be agreed before sensitive intake. An institution’s consent and a maker’s consent each cover only what that person can authorize.

After the fieldwork, return six honest outcomes, including refusals or deferrals; draft stories with evidence and permissions; a cost reconciliation; and a barrier log. These support later itinerary and policy work. They do not automatically create a bookable product or government partnership.
