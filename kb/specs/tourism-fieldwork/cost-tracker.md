# Quote and expense tracker guide

[Download the cost tracker](cost-tracker.xlsx). Use the workbook as a **blank working template for the six-experience option**. The existing ₹59,340 allowance is an estimate, not an approved cash ceiling. Enter the actual ceiling and its decision reference only after a funding decision. Quotes, commitments and payments are different records.

## How to use the workbook

1. Copy the workbook to an approved restricted working location before adding supplier names, quote documents, consent references or receipts. The Git copy stays a blank template. Never add bank details or identity documents.
2. **Budget** shows the existing eight cost assumptions, contingency, quoted amounts and recorded expenditure. Blue inputs are editable. An empty funding ceiling produces no remaining-balance claim.
3. **Quotes** holds alternative supplier offers. Record scope, gross total including taxes, expiry, cancellation terms and a restricted quote reference. Mark at most one quote per scope as Selected for the comparison. Selection does not authorize spending. Use unique scope labels such as Vehicle day 1 or Host P01 to distinguish alternatives from additional work.
4. **Expenses** holds one row per actual obligation, linked to the selected quote where applicable. Enter the agreed gross amount separately from payments to date. Update cumulative payments in that row instead of adding the same commitment again for a deposit. Refunds reduce cumulative net paid; retain the transaction history/receipts in the restricted record.
5. Reconcile against receipts and the approved ceiling before further commitments. Record a zero only when genuinely zero. Empty quotes or expense amounts mean unrecorded information, not free service.

The template reserves 60 quote and 60 expense rows. Formulas cover those rows only; extend formulas and validation deliberately if more are required. Do not overwrite calculated totals. A blank expense log does not prove that no money was spent outside it. The summary labels totals as entered records and leaves missing-stage totals unavailable.

## Review before commitment

Check quote scope, validity, tax-inclusive total, travel/tickets/material inclusions, cancellation/deposit, independent conflicts and the human authorization reference. Retain competing quotes where appropriate. If only one supplier is suitable, document why; no invented three-quote requirement is imposed.

Remaining capacity is approved ceiling minus recorded commitments. Payments are shown separately and are **not added again** to commitments. A negative remaining amount means commitments exceed the ceiling. An unpaid balance means agreed amount less net paid; investigate negative balances and wrong categories. Contingency is a reserve, not a spending authorization or second invoice.
