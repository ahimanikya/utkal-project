# Host and visit confirmation checklist

Use one copy per experience, linked by its existing experience ID. Record the respondent’s role, date and evidence for each answer. **Unknown is a valid answer.** A blank answer is never “yes,” “free,” “open” or “accessible.”

## Identity and permission

- Experience ID, place and specific activity:
- Confirming organisation or host and their authority for this activity:
- Proposed date and time, duration, meeting point and researcher count:
- Invitation/permission to enter or interview: pending / agreed / declined, with scope and date:
- Public credit/name preference; public contact route only if expressly permitted:
- Private evidence reference, kept in the approved restricted home:

## Visit terms

- Opening date/time and exceptions; reconfirmation contact/process:
- Maximum/minimum group and what is included; who provides interpretation:
- Gross INR price, unit and quantity; taxes, materials, tickets and travel included/excluded:
- Quote date, expiry, deposit, cancellation/refund and weather terms:
- No-purchase option; any optional purchase separately priced:
- What the host receives: proposed fee, materials support, attribution or other agreed benefit:

## Access and wellbeing

- Actual approach, walking surface, steps, slopes and distances:
- Drop-off, seating, shade, toilets and drinking water, with observed versus reported facts distinguished:
- Space or timing constraints; sensory/crowd considerations and alternatives:
- Tools, heat or other activity-specific limits explained by the host:
- If food is involved, ingredients, dietary/allergen handling and what the provider cannot guarantee:
- Participant needs considered privately; suitability discussed with that person rather than assigned a universal access label:

## Recording and publication permission

Record **separate** pending / yes / no / restricted answers for notes, direct quotation, audio, identifiable people photographs, workspace photographs, object/design images and website publication. Specify attribution, permitted uses and any expiry or review condition. If a person cannot authorize an image/design, find the rights holder or omit it. Do not record minors without an agreed safeguarding/guardian process; the initial assignment does not include interviewing children.

Ask whether the speaker wants to check the accuracy of their name, quote and description before publication. Explain the correction/withdrawal contact process without promising that already shared copies can be retrieved. No recording by default, no hidden recording and no consent inferred from payment. Send only the agreed excerpt or images for accuracy review.

## Outcome and release

| State | Evidence needed | What it permits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Desk lead | KB reference and unanswered operating questions | Further research only |
| Contact authorized | Named recipient and message approval | That specific enquiry |
| Appointment agreed | Host authority, date, activity and access/fee terms | A separately funded research visit within those terms |
| Locally confirmed | Dated answers, documented restrictions and scoped permission | Internal review; not automatic trial or public listing |
| Trial assessed | Actual trial record and observations | Editorial consideration with limitations |
| Public listing approved | Human editorial approval and suitable public evidence/rights | Only the approved published scope |

Founder-approved spending and a safe, suitable arrangement remain necessary for travel even when a host agrees. If a critical condition is unknown, defer the visit or narrow it with the host; do not infer a full approval from one checked box.

## Handoff record

Record visit outcome (not visited / completed / declined / deferred), what was observed, host-reported statements, sourced facts, contradictions, costs and receipts by restricted reference, next action, human owner and next check date. Reconfirm variable details before a planned group visit; the existing model suggests a 90-day routine recheck, not a guarantee of freshness.
