# Tourism pilot capacity and budget

**Recommend the six-experience verification option first: ₹59,340 (round planning allowance ₹60,000).** The larger twenty-experience option is ₹157,090, approximately ₹1.57 lakh. These are constructed allowances for planning, not market research, supplier quotations, approved spending or prices charged to visitors. Neither option assumes an incentive reimbursement.

The [structured cost sheet](../research/references/data/tourism-pilot-budget.json) contains quantities and arithmetic. Obtain tax-inclusive quotes before commitment; the allowances do not establish current market rates. Any tax or fee above an allowance changes the total. Replace each assumption with the dated quote, inclusions and cancellation terms.

Six visits assume three local field days with a coordinator and researcher: six combined person-days for meals/admissions. Interpretation days may be separate appointments; their fee must include the interpreter’s local incidentals or be re-costed. The access reserve allows a small adjustment, not a promise that every route can accommodate every need. Host allowances are negotiated compensation for time/materials where appropriate; institutional fees follow institutional rules. Unspent allowances are not automatic payments. Avoid duplicate charges if the coordinator also provides interpretation.

The larger option assumes eight field days, some with more than two appointments. This is feasible only after routing and host availability are checked. If two appointments per day is the practical limit, re-cost for at least ten field days before approving expansion.

Current AI assistance is bounded desk support; it neither replaces a field visit nor appoints staff. The editorial allowance is for future human review, not an invoice for this assistant. All human reporting remains to a human. Staff may combine roles only with an explicit, re-costed arrangement.

Allow roughly two–three weeks for the small phase or six–eight for the larger phase after people, appointments and funding are agreed. These are planning windows, not promised delivery dates.

## Funding and boundaries

Proposed payer for the small phase: Founder, only following a separate funding decision. No payer or sponsor is committed. A later Collective or partner contribution would need agreed terms; it must not buy favourable encyclopedia coverage. No new software subscription is required for this desk pack.

The estimate covers local research from a Bhubaneswar base, ordinary evidence capture and review. It excludes travel to Odisha, lodging, public events, visitor tour delivery, specialist production, professional/legal fees and any insurance or additional permits required for delivery. Quote and add these where applicable. Optional photography with hired crew is outside scope; existing equipment and consented reference captures are assumed.

## Decision and stop points

1. Select the first six, appoint a local coordinator, set a cash ceiling and confirm who pays. This documentation does not authorize expenditure.
2. Before each appointment, agree consent, compensation and practical access. Replace unsuitable candidates; preserve their record and reason.
3. Return six completed evidence records or record why fewer were possible, a cost reconciliation and a barrier log. No success quota should pressure a host to participate.
4. Review learning before expanding to twenty. If host participation, access, unit cost or visitor value is weak, revise or stop. Build three itineraries only from sufficiently confirmed records.
