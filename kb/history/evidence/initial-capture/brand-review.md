# Utkal Project · brand and asset review

**Review proposal. Only the project name and tagline below are locked.**

**Utkal Project**  
**Rediscover Utkal. Reimagine Odisha.**

This review brings together the earlier identity concept, saved creative brief and provenance, the current article prototype, and the Bhubaneswar photo archive. It does not approve a final logo, commission an artist, modify the original assets or publish anything. Recommendations are design judgments, not findings from audience testing.

## What the identity needs to do

The name and tagline now represent a broader initiative: knowledge, heritage, belonging and opportunity. The encyclopedia is its first expression. The identity should welcome residents, the diaspora, curious visitors, students, researchers and future collaborators. It should feel credible enough for a sourced article and inviting enough for a personal story.

The earlier brief asked for warmth, artistic character and a local connection. Those qualities remain useful. The updated direction should also make room for contemporary Odisha—people making things, learning, building businesses and shaping their communities. Images of coastlines and historical motifs alone cannot communicate that full ambition.

A useful descriptive line, separate from the locked tagline, is **“A living encyclopedia of Odisha.”** Retain it near an introduction or in discovery copy so unfamiliar visitors understand the first offering. This is a recommendation, not a replacement tagline.

## Logo assessment

The existing emblem combines an open book, sails and a hull. My reading is **knowledge + discovery + connections beyond the shore**. That makes it a plausible fit for the encyclopedia and the broader project. This symbolism is a contemporary design interpretation, not a claim that the mark is an inherited historical emblem.

The main weakness is distinctiveness: book and boat symbols can also suggest publishing, education or travel. The execution will need its own recognizable proportions and lettering. The current broad hull and narrow internal page lines need testing at small sizes. Neither distinctiveness testing nor small-size validation has been completed by this review.

The earlier images also show two different versions: red pages with a teal hull on the board, and an all-red standalone mark. These should become controlled variants of one resolved master, not two independently drawn logos.

Recommended next logo comparison:

1. **Evolved book-and-boat — preferred:** simplify the emblem and pair it with a well-spaced Utkal Project wordmark. Balance heritage with clarity.
2. **Wordmark-led alternative:** expressive but readable Utkal Project lettering, with a restrained standalone symbol. Test whether this better expresses the initiative's future-facing ambition.

Compare them on the same website header, square social avatar and article-share card. Choose based on legibility, recognition and fit with both knowledge and opportunity. No new logo artwork is generated or adopted in this review.

Recommended production variants after selection: horizontal mark and name; stacked mark and name; name with tagline; icon only; one-colour; reversed for dark surfaces. Keep the tagline as live text on the website and omit it in tiny avatars and favicons. The name should remain readable without the tagline.

## Colour and typography

| Colour | Token | Proposed use |
|---|---|---|
| Deep red | #8F2435 | Brand, emphasis and primary actions |
| Warm ivory | #F5EBDD | Reading surfaces |
| Charcoal | #24211F | Body text and data |
| Lagoon teal | #245C59 | Links and secondary emphasis |
| Ochre | #C9923E | Restrained decorative accents |

The saved contrast report gives charcoal/ivory 13.57:1, red/ivory 7.21:1, teal/ivory 6.48:1 and ochre/ivory 2.32:1. Keep ochre out of small text on ivory. These are the existing token calculations, not a new whole-site accessibility audit.

The literary serif plus plain body type is a good starting point for long-form knowledge. Georgia and system sans-serif are prototype choices; generated logo lettering is not a production font specification. Choose final type and verify permitted uses when producing the identity master. An Odia wordmark and bilingual typesetting need their own editorial and design review.

In the current header, the tagline becomes tiny, widely spaced uppercase text on narrow screens. My recommendation is sentence case, less tracking and a larger size—approximately 12–14 CSS pixels to start testing. Preserve the approved wording exactly. Let generous spacing and readable type carry the warmth; avoid making every surface look like aged parchment.

## Photography and illustration

Use photography to establish the actual people and places behind the project. The selected Bhubaneswar sequence has a personal account, location context and archive history that the generated landscape cannot provide. Preserve whole artwork compositions where appropriate, ordinary street context, and the separation between photographer credit and artist attribution.

The archive identifies the photographer as Ahimanikya Satapathy and notes that mural artists are not yet identified. Its camera metadata records February 2009 for the art collection. Keep these qualifications attached to publication candidates. Documentary publication decisions should not automatically authorize adapting a mural into a logo, pattern or merchandise design.

The AI-generated coastal scene and floral ornament are useful creative references. They should remain clearly distinguished from practitioner-made work and documentary imagery. A future artist collaboration can develop locally specific illustrations with explicit credit and an agreed scope. It is not a prerequisite for completing the first website prototype.

Broaden the eventual image library to include everyday life, makers, learning, contemporary work, landscapes and diaspora connections. Those are collection priorities, not assets already available in this review.

## Brand relationships

Use **Utkal Project** and its locked tagline for the master initiative. Treat “encyclopedia” as a product description. Utkal Blueprint, Utkal Collective and Utkal Unbound can share visual principles while retaining separate names, lifecycle and governance. BAL and BAC remain deferred.

Do not automatically put the same emblem or the phrase “A Utkal Project initiative” on every future entity. The exact endorsement wording and visual relationships should reflect the relationship the Founder approves for each. A common operating blueprint does not by itself establish a common commercial identity.

## Recommended next decision

Keep the existing palette and editorial warmth as the leading direction. Develop the **book-and-boat** concept and one **wordmark-led** alternative under the approved name/tagline. Review those on real website and social applications before choosing the production logo. This review recommends that comparison; it does not lock the palette, symbol or typography on the Founder's behalf.
