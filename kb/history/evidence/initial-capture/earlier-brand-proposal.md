# Utkala — identity concept 01

**Recommended name: Utkala**  
**Descriptor: A living encyclopedia of Odisha**

A warm editorial identity that makes room for Odisha's people, places, food, making traditions and growth. This is a proposal for the independent community encyclopedia, not an official state identity.

![Identity concept board](identity-board.png)

## The name

Use **Utkala** as the memorable cultural name. Keep **Odisha** in the descriptor, page titles and search descriptions so a visitor immediately understands the subject. Utkala is the user's preferred cultural expression; this branding does not assert identical historical and modern territorial boundaries.

| Candidate | Strength | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| **Utkala — recommended** | Short, expressive and broad enough for tourism, culture and trade | Needs the Odisha descriptor for unfamiliar audiences |
| Utkala Commons | Makes the shared contribution model explicit | Longer and more institutional |
| Odisha Atlas | Immediately describes a reference resource | Map-centred and descriptive; already appears in publication contexts |

Preliminary discovery on 27 September 2026 found existing use of the root in [Utkalamrita](https://utkalamrita.com/en-us/pages/about-us), the registered publication title [Utkala Sanskara](https://prgi.gov.in/registration-title-details-data/0c24d6f6-e74b-11ee-ab0e-00155d022d0b), and a [Census administrative atlas of Odisha](https://censusindia.gov.in/nada/index.php/catalog/42/study-description). This is a creative shortlist, not a finding of name or domain availability. Resolve the final name before spending on production masters or signage.

## The symbol

An **open book becomes a boat**: knowledge, discovery and a contemporary reference to the maritime theme already researched in the KB. It can accompany a maker's story, a destination guide or a growth chart. It is an original design proposal, not a recovered historical emblem.

The [standalone logo concept](logo-concept.png) shows the single-colour direction. The board explores a two-colour variation with a teal hull. Both are raster concepts; a designer should resolve the curves and wordmark into one vector master, an icon-only mark, an inverse version and a small-size variant after selection. The generated lettering does not identify a licensed production font. Check the icon at 16, 24 and 32 pixels during that work.

## Colour system

| Colour | Exact token | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Deep red | `#8F2435` | Identity, major headings and primary actions |
| Warm ivory | `#F5EBDD` | Main reading surface and open space |
| Charcoal | `#24211F` | Body text and information |
| Ochre | `#C9923E` | Small ornaments and highlights |
| Lagoon teal | `#245C59` | Links, secondary actions and selected data series |

Start with roughly 70% ivory, 20% dark ink/red and 10% teal/ochre across a typical editorial page; photographs need not follow this ratio. These are project colours, not a claim about one official or historically definitive Odisha palette. Generated pictures approximate these colours; use the exact [CSS tokens](tokens.css) in the website.

Use charcoal, red or teal text on ivory. Use ivory text on red or teal buttons. Keep ochre out of small text on ivory; use it decoratively or pair it with charcoal. The [contrast report](contrast.json) calculates these token pairs; it is not a complete accessibility audit. Underline text links, label chart series directly and do not communicate data status through colour alone.

## A local artistic character

Use generous ivory reading areas, literary headings, plain readable body text and a small number of expressive illustrations. A prototype can use Georgia and the system sans-serif; commission or select licensed production typefaces later. Have an Odia editor and designer review Odia typography and bilingual layouts.

Treat Odisha's traditions as distinct bodies of work with named practitioners. Pattachitra-informed narrative composition can guide an illustrated story opening. Textile stories can show the particular maker's actual weave. Appliqué stories can show real material, stitching and colour. Do not apply a generic pattern everywhere and call it authentic Sambalpuri, Pattachitra or Pipili work.

The present artwork is **AI-generated contemporary concept art**, created with the built-in image generator. It is not artisan-made Pattachitra. Boats, cloth, food vessels, architecture and scenery are imaginative; they must not illustrate a factual claim about a specific place, textile or historical vessel. The coastal scene communicates an art direction and needs local specificity in commissioned work.

## Where to invest next

First settle the name and book-and-boat direction. Then fund a resolved vector identity and a paid collaboration with Odisha artists: one distinctive hero, a restrained ornament set and a small series of story illustrations. Invest alongside that in credited photography of actual makers, dishes and places. These will give the finished site local detail that the present concepts cannot supply.

For each commission, agree the creative fee, permitted adaptations and uses, source-file delivery, credit placement and the artist's review of the published context. No commission, outreach, name registration or public launch has been undertaken in this concept edition.
