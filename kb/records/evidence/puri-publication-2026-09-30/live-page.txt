    - paragraph: 04 · Before you go
    - heading "Leave space for a living town" [level=2]
    - paragraph: The tourism beach guide advises consulting local Nulia lifeguards before entering the sea. Follow the day’s restrictions and conditions.
    - paragraph: Choose a smaller number of stops during busy festival periods. Confirm transport and access directly; no opening hours, bathing conditions or festival schedule are guaranteed here.
    - paragraph: Ask before photographing worship, work or private homes. For an outdoor day, plan shade, drinking water and a route back that suits everyone in your group.
    - heading "Choose your season and your kind of visit" [level=3]
    - paragraph: Incredible India suggests October–February for Puri. Check the forecast for your dates and decide whether you want a festival visit or a less crowded period. Neither the season nor a photograph guarantees quiet conditions.
    - heading "Let the maker set the pace" [level=3]
    - paragraph: Ask whether a visit is convenient before entering a workshop. Agree any demonstration fee, ask who made the work, and request permission before recording a story. Leave space for a refusal.
    - heading "Plan the connections, not just the stops" [level=3]
    - paragraph: Discuss the exact pickup point, waiting arrangements and return journey with your driver or transport provider. Keep Raghurajpur and Konark as separate choices until the distances and your available time work together.

      - heading "Leave space between shore and town" [level=2]
      - paragraph: A few prompts for your own encounter.
      - paragraph: Choose a moment to look, listen and stop moving. Notice the difference between the place in your photograph and the place around you.
      - heading "Before you go" [level=3]
      - paragraph: Let the temple, a meal and the shore have their own time. Incredible India suggests October–February for Puri; choose your dates around the weather and the kind of visit you want. Leave room for a queue, a conversation and the journey back.
      - heading "Temple visit · check before joining the queue" [level=3]
      - paragraph: Confirm entry eligibility, dress and belongings rules with the temple administration before leaving your accommodation. Ask specifically about phones, cameras, leather items and bags, and where permitted storage is available. We could not confirm the current rules from the administration website. Check the entrance, access assistance and ritual interruptions for your date; no entry or waiting time is promised here.
      - heading "Mahaprasad · begin with its meaning" [level=3]
      - paragraph: Odisha Tourism describes Mahaprasad as the offering to Lord Jagannath at Puri. Approach it as part of a devotional tradition. Ask how it is shared, what participation is appropriate and where to collect it under the current arrangements. Confirm availability and any payment directly; a restaurant meal is not automatically temple Mahaprasad.
      - heading "At the table · mustard, prawns or something sweet" [level=3]
      - paragraph: The official Puri guide names machha besara, fish in mustard gravy; chingudi tarkari, a prawn curry; and chhena poda, a cheese sweet. Choose what suits your table. Ask the kitchen about today’s menu, ingredients, dietary needs and price. These are regional food leads, not reviewed restaurants or claims of Puri origin.
      - heading "Choose your shore moment" [level=3]
      - paragraph: For a morning pause, consider a shore walk and sunrise; Odisha Tourism describes an evening beach market too. Pick an exact meeting point and ask about the approach, toilets and return pickup. Consult the local Nulia lifeguards before entering the sea and follow the day’s restrictions. A beach name, photograph or seasonal recommendation cannot establish safe conditions.
      - heading "Arrive with a return plan" [level=3]
      - paragraph: Puri district identifies Puri railway station for the temple and Biju Patnaik International Airport in Bhubaneswar for air arrival. Check your actual train or flight, then arrange the onward transfer separately. Agree the destination, fare and pickup point with the transport provider; keep the return details somewhere you can use without your phone. Current transfers, fares and journey times are not verified here.
      - heading "Where to ask" [level=3]
      - paragraph:
        - text: For local orientation, use the Puri office in Odisha Tourism’s tourist-office directory. Ask how to reach the temple administration for current visitor rules. Confirm meals with the host or kitchen, and shore conditions with lifeguards on the day.
