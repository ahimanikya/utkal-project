Konark Sun Temple
Make the Sun Temple the focus, then decide whether your day has room for Chandrabhaga’s shore. A festival visit needs a different plan: confirm the current programme before arranging your stay.
Confirm local access, transport and availability before travel.

Connect with care: Ask a guide to distinguish a carving’s interpretation from a story told about its making. Both can be interesting without being the same kind of evidence.
Before you go: Check current entry arrangements and your return journey. If an event draws you here, confirm its current programme before planning around it.
Leave room for: Let a coastal meal become a pause rather than another landmark. Read the food guide, then ask a kitchen what is actually available.

Puri
Begin with Puri’s coast, Jagannath traditions and Raghurajpur’s craft, then choose the pace that suits you.
Confirm local access, transport and availability before travel.
Choose your season and your kind of visit: Incredible India suggests October–February for Puri. Check the forecast for your dates and decide whether you want a festival visit or a less crowded period. Neither the season nor a photograph guarantees quiet conditions.
Let the maker set the pace: Ask whether a visit is convenient before entering a workshop. Agree any demonstration fee, ask who made the work, and request permission before recording a story. Leave space for a refusal.
Plan the connections, not just the stops: Discuss the exact pickup point, waiting arrangements and return journey with your driver or transport provider. Keep Raghurajpur and Konark as separate choices until the distances and your available time work together.
Notice: Choose a moment to look, listen and stop moving. Notice the difference between the place in your photograph and the place around you.
Connect with care: Ask before photographing people or devotional activity. Let the person you speak with decide whether their story may be shared.
Before you go: Check access rules for the places you choose and plan your return. At the shore, seek current local guidance; this guide does not establish safe swimming conditions.
Leave room for: Read about Raghurajpur before visiting a workshop. Plan the transfer and approach the encounter as time with a maker.
Bring home a memory: Remember a sound, a conversation or a pause as well as a view. Keep private encounters out of a public post without permission.
