Chandipur’s changing shore

Place · Balasore

Low tide exposes a broad stretch of seabed. Odisha Tourism advises following local guidance as the water returns.

Stay within locally advised limits. This is a tidal landscape, not a promise of safe swimming.

Mayurbhanj · Check local access and transport. Travel times have not been calculated.

Similipal: a forest day

Place · Mayurbhanj

The reserve generally admits visitors from 1 November to 15 June; actual dates depend on official notices and conditions.

Confirm your permit, entry gate, vehicle and permitted route before travelling. No wildlife sighting is guaranteed.

Mudhi Mansa in Baripada

Food · Mayurbhanj

Puffed rice and meat curry make Baripada’s well-known Mudhi Mansa pairing.

Ask about the meat, spice level and current price. Vegetarian travellers can ask the same kitchen what it can prepare separately.
