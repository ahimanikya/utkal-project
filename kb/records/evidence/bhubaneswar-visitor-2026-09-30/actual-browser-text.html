Choose the season

Odisha Tourism recommends November–March for Bhubaneswar and describes June–September as the rainy period. These are seasonal guides, not a forecast for your dates. In warmer weather, leave the middle of the day for a meal and a rest.

Give lunch its own place

Begin with dalma: lentils cooked with vegetables and spices. The tourism food guide also pairs it with pitha or vada. Ask what is being cooked that day, how it is served and which ingredients are used. Follow your appetite into the food stories, then choose a kitchen locally.

Arrange the return, too

The official Dhauli guide lists Bhubaneswar’s airport and railway station as arrival points and suggests a taxi or auto for the site. Agree the return pickup, waiting charge and exact stops before leaving. This is a transport starting point, not a live service or fare quote.

Meet a person, not a performance

Ask a willing guide what a carving means to them; agree a guiding fee before starting. Ask a cook what makes today’s meal different. Request permission before recording or photographing people, and accept a refusal. Confirm entry, worship and photography rules separately at each monument.

Ask about access before you commit

Step-free routes, toilets, shade, seating and named hosts have not been field-checked here. Describe your specific needs to the venue or operator, including the route from drop-off to the entrance. Choose a different stop if the arrangements do not work for you.
