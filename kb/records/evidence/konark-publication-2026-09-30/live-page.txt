      - heading "Before you go" [level=3]
      - paragraph: Give the monument and the museum their own space in your day. Start with a close look at the temple’s carvings, then decide whether you want to follow those details into the museum. Check each visit separately and arrange the return journey before setting out.
      - heading "Sun Temple · plan for daylight" [level=3]
      - paragraph: ASI lists monument access from sunrise to sunset. Incredible India lists a later closing time, so confirm the hours for your date with ASI rather than relying on evening entry. Look for one carved detail before trying to see everything.
      - heading "Archaeological museum · a separate visit" [level=3]
      - paragraph: "The museum gives some of the site’s recovered sculptures another setting: ASI identifies Balarama, Varaha and Trivikrama figures from the smaller brick temple in its collection. Treat the museum as a separate stop. Its current hours, closure day and visitor facilities have not yet been verified by us."
      - heading "Choose the right ticket listing" [level=3]
      - paragraph: On the ticket portal linked by ASI, choose the Bhubaneshwar circle. Sun Temple, Konark and Archaeological Site Museum, Konark appear as separate choices. Check the venue, date, visitor category and current amount before paying; do not assume one selection covers both visits.
