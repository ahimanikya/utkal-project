  - article:
    - paragraph: 01 · A closer look
    - heading "A modern monument in an older landscape" [level=2]
    - paragraph: "The white dome catches the eye first. Odisha Tourism dates the Shanti Stupa to 1972 and credits the Japan Buddha Sangha and Kalinga Nippon Buddha Sangha with its construction. Give this modern peace monument its own moment, then look for the older messages in the landscape. Seeing both changes the visit: the memorial and the evidence have different stories to tell."
    - paragraph: 02 · A closer look
    - heading "Before the dome, there were words" [level=2]
    - paragraph: The National Monuments Authority dates the inscription site to the third century BCE and identifies its language as Prakrit, written in Brahmi script. Dhauli has Major Rock Edicts I–X and XIV, alongside two Separate Rock Edicts; it does not contain the complete set of fourteen. Ask a guide which inscription a translation belongs to. Even without reading the letters, you can notice the extraordinary choice to address people through stone.
    - paragraph: 03 · A closer look
    - heading "An elephant emerging from stone" [level=2]
    - paragraph: The elephant’s front half rises out of the rock above the inscriptions, as the National Monuments Authority’s description records. Follow the meeting of carved form and uncarved stone in this photograph. On a visit, look from the permitted viewing area; a good photograph does not need a hand on the carving. Let the older sculpture and the white dome remain two distinct images in your memory.

      - img "The front of an elephant carved from a large rock, with trees behind it."
      - generic: The elephant carved from rock at Dhauli · AmlanTheTramp, January 2017.
    - paragraph: 04 · A closer look
    - heading "A story carried by the Daya" [level=2]
    - paragraph: "Odisha Tourism retells the popular legend of the Daya turning red during the Kalinga War. It is a powerful remembered image of loss, not a measured fact about the river. You can listen to the story and still ask what the inscriptions actually say. The question in this landscape is large enough to take home: how should power care for the people it governs?"
    - paragraph: 05 · A closer look
    - heading "An evening is a separate plan" [level=2]
    - paragraph: "The tourism department describes a light-and-sound show and the Dhauli-Kalinga festival of music and dance. An overview is not a programme for your travel date. Confirm whether an event is running, its language, start and finish, tickets and cancellation arrangements before planning around it. A daylight visit can stand on its own: leave time to look and ask, rather than making the whole stop depend on a show."
    - paragraph: 06 · A closer look
    - heading "Leave room for the way back" [level=2]
    - paragraph: "Agree which places you want to see with your driver or guide: the inscription site and the Peace Pagoda need their own approach checks. Ask about drop-off points, walking surfaces, steps, seating, shade and toilets. Arrange a return pickup, especially for an evening visit, and leave a meal break in your day. Keep worship spaces quiet and follow current photography and footwear guidance. Specific access routes, facilities and services have not been verified by Utkal."
    - region "Make room for a quieter question":
      - paragraph: Make it your visit
      - heading "Make room for a quieter question" [level=2]
      - paragraph: A few prompts for your own encounter.
      - paragraph: Look for the difference between a monument you can see and an older story you have come to understand. Resist fitting every layer into one photograph.
      - heading "Before you go" [level=3]
      - paragraph: Give the rock inscriptions and the 1972 Peace Pagoda separate attention. Agree both stops and their approaches with your driver or guide. Confirm return transport and any show for your actual date; a general tourism listing is not a booking.
      - heading "Read the older layer" [level=3]
      - paragraph: Dhauli’s inscription site dates to the third century BCE; the text is Prakrit in Brahmi script. Ask for a clearly identified translation and notice the elephant carved above the inscriptions. Stay in permitted viewing areas and leave the stone untouched.
      - heading "Give the Peace Pagoda its own moment" [level=3]
      - paragraph: The white Shanti Stupa was built in 1972. Treat it as a modern peace monument, distinct from the Ashokan inscriptions. Ask your guide to distinguish historical evidence, later remembrance and the local Daya river legend.
      - heading "Confirm the evening before keeping it free" [level=3]
      - paragraph: Odisha Tourism mentions a light-and-sound show and a music-and-dance festival. Check a dated programme, language, ticket arrangements, finish time and cancellations with the organiser. No performance, price or current schedule is confirmed here.
      - heading "Plan both approaches and your return" [level=3]
      - paragraph: "Ask separately about the inscription site and pagoda: exact drop-off, walking surfaces, steps, seating, shade and toilets. Agree driver waiting and return pickup before travelling, including after an evening event. Build in water and a meal break; current facilities and accessible routes remain unverified."
      - heading "Where to ask" [level=3]
      - paragraph:
        - text: Look for the Bhubaneswar office in Odisha Tourism’s tourist-office directory. Ask where to confirm Dhauli’s current show programme and visitor access.
