# Vector refinement 03 and Sea & Stone merchandise

**Review candidate · no final design acceptance or production release**

Founder responded “OK” to the recommendation to finalise the logo and four merchandise designs, then clarified: “The store merchandise needs to be designed”. This authorizes design development and a concrete review, not an inference that the unseen result is accepted.

Thirteen native SVG brand candidates retain the book-and-boat idea: separate English/Odia signatures in colour, sea, earth and reverse; four symbol colour variants; a simplified small symbol. All lettering is outlined using the bundled Noto Serif Oriya Bold font, with CoreText shaping. No fallback fonts were used. Original font and licence remain unchanged. Reproducible path data and the vector generator live in `projects/site/design/`; website assets in `projects/site/public/assets/brand-v3/`.

Proposed use: colour signature on warm paper; true one-colour reverse on dark backgrounds; simplified icon at 24 px. Keep at least one-quarter symbol-width of clear space, preserve aspect ratio, and check actual size before print. Suggested minimum horizontal signature widths: 180 px English / 120 px Odia on screen. These are provisional usage recommendations, not measured manufacturing limits. Palette and narrative remain in [direction 02](brand-direction-02.md).

The store now presents a **Sea & Stone** collection proposal: black tee with Odia emblem/wordmark; natural jhula bag with English UTKAL and a laterite sun; ivory coffee cup with sea-blue handle and wave band; matte sea bottle with Odia lettering and straw wave band. Only the black T-shirt base and four-product range are confirmed; finishes, placements and collection name are proposals.

Four built-in image-generation mockups show surface and material intent. Four separately drawn vector artwork studies provide outlined lettering and proposed print canvases. Generated mockups and vectors differ in details; reconcile the chosen look into one exact vendor-template proof before manufacturing. Images are not supplier photos or evidence of material, capacity or quality. Botanical motifs remain future researched work.

Creator and final editor: Ahimanikya Satapathy. Current assistant: vector/code development, layout, prompts and AI-assisted mockups. Noto contributors retain font credit. No vendor has been contacted. Review assets, licence, specification questions and a brief are downloadable from the Store design-review page; its independent KB holds the full vendor brief and prompt record.
