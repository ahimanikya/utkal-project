# Odisha living-heritage and sensory identity

**Research shortlist and design proposal · not approved symbols or product artwork**

The Founder asked for an Odisha identity people can recognize and carry into daily life, starting with Utkal-branded objects. Build a family of place-based stories rather than treating any one plant as exclusive to Odisha. The proposed name for this strand is **Odisha Living-Heritage & Sensory Identity**; it is a branch of the broader heritage taxonomy, not proof of ancestry or historical influence.

| Candidate | Evidence and limits | Proposed creative use |
|---|---|---|
| Kia / Kewda · କିଆ | The official GI registry lists **Ganjam Kewda Flower**, registered for Odisha. This is the strongest geographically specific lead here. | Fragrant coastal gardens; long leaves and flower forms after botanical reference review. |
| Bakula · ବକୁଳ | Odisha Review documents Satyabadi’s Bakula Bana and educational history. Bakula is associated with Mimusops elengi; do not confuse it with mango blossom called baula in some contexts. | A story about learning, shade and gathering, connected to the encyclopedia’s purpose. |
| Champa · ଚମ୍ପା | Utkal University’s Man in Society (2017), Table VI, records Swarna Champa among flowers in a specific Dongaria Kondh context. This is not evidence of one universal Odisha custom. | A fragrant flower study, developed with local botanical and cultural review. |
| Amba · ଆମ୍ବ (mango) | ORMAS describes Gaon Amba Pasara supporting local mango marketing. Mango is shared across many regions. | Seasonal fruit and grower stories; named local varieties only after verification. |
| Panasa · ପଣସ (jackfruit) | Odisha Agriculture’s 2024 annual report documents a Jackfruit Mission. That supports a contemporary livelihood connection, not exclusivity. | Fruit, food and livelihood stories; a bold texture study for later collections. |

**Recommendation:** begin research/art direction with Ganjam Kia and Satyabadi Bakula; explore Champa after choosing the actual species. Mango and jackfruit can anchor seasonal stories later. The first four store products remain Utkal wordmark concepts; none of these plant motifs has been approved for production.

Champa needs particular care: Magnolia champaca (also known as Michelia champaca) and Plumeria/frangipani are different plants. Vernacular usage varies. The Founder’s Bali comparison is inspiration for recognizable identity, not evidence that Odisha and Bali share an identical flower tradition.

## Knowledge-to-work record

For each candidate collect: local names and scripts; botanical identity and reference image; district/community; season and sensory qualities; documented uses; specific claim with sources and inspection status; testimony/interpretation distinction; artwork creator, licence and credit; review status; approved uses; related stories/products. Plant photographs are not included yet. Commission or license appropriate visual references before production design.

Next work: verify the limited-access sources, ask knowledgeable local contributors to review names and regional meanings, obtain credited botanical references, then prepare a small illustrated study for Founder selection. Avoid assigning sacred or community-specific meaning to generic merchandise without informed collaboration.
