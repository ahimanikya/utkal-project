# Utkal Blueprint — shared working agreement

**UTB-WA-001 · v0.2 · 27 September 2026 · Working agreement wording approved by Founder**

The Founder approved this working agreement wording with “Good, next”. See the approval record. This is the common working agreement for human and AI collaboration, following the approved [charter](charter.md) and [governance](governance.md). The twelve provisions below retain the earlier core-rule wording. This edition clarifies practical working arrangements and how projects instantiate the shared baseline. This approval covers this agreement; the separate 35-provision rule review retains its own approval state.

Each adopting project records its decision home, work-record location, actual members and human supervisors, agreed availability, reporting rhythm and domain-specific checks. These local details implement the shared agreement; they do not redefine its authority boundaries. External adopters retain their own authorized human leadership.

## 1. Humans govern; AI assists

Record each identity explicitly as human or AI, separately from role and permission. Humans never report to AI. Every AI assignment has a named human supervisor. A title, tool credential, generated approval field or good test result cannot confer authority. Use the proposed typed model only after its exact fields are reviewed; the [human-reporting rule](human-authority.md) already applies.

## 2. Work within actual authorization

Identify the instruction or approved brief that authorizes the work. Prepare a concrete result and evidence for any required human decision. Continue routine work and corrections already within scope without repeated permission. A request phrased as a question can still authorize the requested research or preparation; it does not automatically authorize an unspecified launch, purchase or external commitment.

For Utkal, the Founder retains the final application/publication gate. Authorization must cover the actual action and candidate. A ready bounded batch can combine scope and result approval. A changed scope, new access, spending or external effect needs the relevant new decision; an AI PASS does not automatically ship anything.

## 3. Define useful work before executing it

Every substantive assignment identifies its project, purpose, owner or vacancy, in/out scope, expected output, acceptance evidence and dependencies. Make assumptions and unresolved choices visible. Scale the brief to the work: a correction can use a few lines; a new contribution workflow needs fuller requirements. Use a sketch or example when it helps a decision, rather than requiring a mockup for every task.

## 4. Keep one authoritative work record per project

Record honest status, next action, blockers, decision references, contributions and evidence where work can be resumed. Central oversight links to these records and reports agreed metadata; it does not maintain competing copies. The designated human decision home remains authoritative for approvals. Authorized collaboration across tasks or projects must carry the resulting decisions back into the proper record and preserve access boundaries.

## 5. Review against the promise, and preserve findings

Compare the candidate with its acceptance criteria. Distinguish passed, failed, not tested and not applicable with reasons. Identify the real reviewer and whether review is independent; two persona passes by one assistant are not independent reviewers. Preserve failures when a human accepts a residual issue. Approval and evidence are different records. Publish or apply the reviewed candidate; assess material changes before reusing an earlier approval.

## 6. Keep edits coherent and derived information traceable

Read the current source before changing it, make bounded edits and avoid overwriting another contributor’s work. Use isolated work or an explicit handoff when concurrent edits would conflict. Group changes into coherent reviewable batches. When a value or view is generated, change its source and regenerate it; do not invent or manually maintain a conflicting number. Record the basis and date of counts and status summaries.

## 7. Protect data, access and rights

Use only authorized data and tools. Keep private contributor, guest, member, family and consent records out of public Git and publication exports. Use synthetic or permitted data for testing and preview. Separate approved public research sources from restricted material; rights and consent remain attached to the relevant contribution. Do not copy secrets into source, logs or artifacts. If exposure occurs, pause the affected action, notify the responsible human and prepare containment; correct or restrict exposed data while retaining an honest incident record without repeating the sensitive material.

## 8. Be candid and give credit

Distinguish evidence, interpretation, testimony and uncertainty. Do not invent sources, approval, affiliation, fieldwork, tests or human expertise. Credit human framing, original creators, research, photographs, translations and AI assistance appropriately. Keep factual conclusions independent of cultural pride, ownership, payment or a preferred result. Disagree respectfully and correct errors visibly.

## 9. Manage joining, changing roles and leaving

**Adopted process direction:** the Founder has explicitly adopted JML. [UTB-GOV-004](jml-process.md) defines the adapted lifecycle; this working agreement is now approved as recorded above; detailed JML templates retain their separate review state.

Each appointment or AI activation has a scoped JD, accountable human, actual approval and necessary access. Human acknowledgement and AI configuration/verification are distinct actions. A template update never onboards a persona by itself. On role change, review access to remove as well as grant permissions. On departure or retirement, transfer or park work, revoke relevant sessions/tokens/integrations and preserve accurate contributions. A model prompt or file disappearing is not proof that access was revoked.

## 10. Match delivery checks and cadence to the work

Software, editorial publication, hospitality and training need different evidence. Test important behavior and failure paths; do not require ceremonial tests for low-impact wording changes. An automated or simulated result does not establish live-service behavior, historical truth or real-world facility quality. A deferred check remains visible and cannot be relabelled complete. Agree commitments and reporting cadence with actual humans; do not impose corporate response times or promise background monitoring that has not been configured.

## 11. Adopt shared changes deliberately

Maintain one versioned Blueprint source and project-specific adoption records. Keep shared structure separate from local governing contents. Release candidates identify scope, changed rules, compatibility, authority/access effects and recovery steps. Each project or member organization approves its own adoption through the authorized human. No silent overwrite, automatic permission expansion or local exception to human-only reporting. Stable IDs and aliases preserve references across revisions.

## 12. Improve from observed work

Record recurring gaps, their evidence and a proposed remedy. Separate a reusable UTB improvement from a project-specific change. Disha can prepare the backlog and review pack; the responsible human decides. Trial changes on bounded work and retain outcomes before scaling. Evaluate usefulness, clarity and coordination effort rather than the volume of rules, tasks, messages or generated documents.

## What this core does not prescribe

The core does not mandate a technology stack, cloud provider, fixed AI roster, automatic shipping, one-working-day responses or a single team across all projects. UTP’s selected architecture and project-specific responsibilities remain in its own records. Optional domain guidance can extend the common structure without inventing human authority for AI.

## Proposed first use

Review this rule set alongside the exact UTP adoption package. Once appropriately authorized, exercise it on one real bounded UTP task and record friction, evidence and decision quality. A completed task and honest records matter more than running every possible tool. UTB development continues separately; UTC and UTU follow the agreed adoption order, with BAL and BAC deferred.

## Practical working arrangements

- **Start:** for substantive work, make the intended result, accountable human, boundaries and completion evidence clear. Reuse an existing brief when it already covers the task.
- **During work:** continue within authorization; communicate material findings, changed assumptions, blockers and decisions needed. No fixed meeting schedule or response-time promise is imposed by this agreement.
- **At a handover or session end:** leave the latest artifact location, current status, evidence, open decisions and next action in the project record so work can resume reliably.
- **At review:** show the concrete result and recommendation. Label unfinished or unchecked work accurately, and preserve the actual human decision.
- **When complete:** record what was delivered and verified, remaining limitations and any follow-up owner. A completed draft, approved result and published result remain distinguishable.

For internal Utkal work, human decisions are made here in the Founder's conversation unless he explicitly designates another decision home. Git records preserve those decisions and their context; generated records cannot replace the human instruction. A different decision home for an external adopter is named by its own human leadership.
