# Utkal Blueprint — authorship and reuse boundary

**Founder-directed statement · 27 September 2026**

**Utkal Blueprint — created by Ahimanikya Satapathy, Founder & Editor-in-Chief of Utkal Project.**

Ahimanikya defines the model's purpose, principles and direction and retains final human authority over its adoption and development. AI provides research, drafting, organization and implementation assistance under his direction; that assistance does not confer creator status or governing authority.

## Discuss reuse before incorporating it

Do not copy or adapt text, code, role definitions, organizational structures or operating mechanisms from another operating model into Utkal without first discussing the specific proposed reuse with Ahimanikya and obtaining his approval. Permission to inspect or learn from material is not permission to incorporate it.

For a proposed reuse, explain the concrete idea, its usefulness to Utkal and the proposed wording or implementation for discussion. Do not silently incorporate it or treat generic approval of a workstream as consent to reuse external material. Existing review drafts remain proposals; this direction does not retrospectively approve them.

## Model attribution

Use the creator statement above in Utkal Blueprint's overview, definition and rule-review documents. Do not add external operating-model credits, acknowledgements, co-creator claims or claims that Ahimanikya created or owns another model. Record actual future contributions separately without changing the founding attribution.

This statement concerns authorship of the organizational model. Sources and contributor credits for encyclopedia research, photographs, translations and other knowledge content retain their own accurate attribution.

## Decision scope

The Founder explicitly directed the authorship statement and discussion-before-reuse boundary in this conversation. Recording them does not adopt the full proposed rule package, activate personas, change access or deploy anything.
