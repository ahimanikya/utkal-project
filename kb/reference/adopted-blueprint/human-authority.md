# Humans report only to humans

**UTB-GOV-001 · Founder-directed principle v0.1 · 27 September 2026**

The Founder explicitly directed: **“Humans will never report to an AI persona”.** Source: this Utkal planning conversation. This rule is confirmed direction, not a pending design option. Its documentation does not imply that technical enforcement has been implemented.

## Governing rule

**No human may have an AI persona, agent, model or automated system as their manager, supervisor or reporting authority.** This applies to direct, indirect, functional, dotted-line, temporary, acting and delegated reporting arrangements. Human organizational reporting chains contain humans only. The top accountable human has no invented superior or self-reporting relationship.

Every AI assignment is accountable to a named human supervisor. AI assists human work; it does not occupy a human leadership vacancy or gain managerial authority through a project-manager title, coordination role, central fleet service or tool permission.

The rule applies throughout Utkal Blueprint and every internal or member-organization instance adopting it. Local charters, role definitions, exceptions and managed-service terms cannot waive it. Independent member organizations retain their own human leadership; this principle does not make them report to Utkal personnel by default.

## Coordination without human supervision authority

An AI coordinator may maintain plans and records, organize authorized work, relay human-approved requests, summarize updates, identify dependencies and prepare recommendations for a human decision-maker. A human giving information to an AI assistant or updating a shared work board is not reporting to that AI as a manager; accountability remains with the named human.

An AI cannot issue its own binding managerial instructions, set a human’s commitments, approve leave, evaluate employment consequences, appoint or remove people, discipline anyone or become the final decision-maker. It may help a human prepare relevant work within authorized scope without inheriting that human’s authority.

For Disha, **AI Project Coordinator** means coordinating the work records and handoffs under the Founder’s direction. Human contributors report to their named human manager, never to Disha. The same boundary holds if the role is later called AI Project Manager.

## Representation and review

Every org chart, roster, JD and reporting record must distinguish human reporting lines from AI assistance and task handoffs. Before adopting a project profile or changing its team, check that:

- Each human reporting authority is an actual human, with no AI inserted anywhere in the managerial chain.
- Each AI assignment has a named accountable human and zero human reports of any kind.
- Collaboration links and central service relationships do not imply managerial authority over people.
- No local exception, title, temporary cover arrangement or delegated workflow overrides this rule.

If a proposed arrangement violates the rule, correct the arrangement rather than approving an exception. Record the finding and retain the last valid human reporting structure. These are documented review criteria; any future automated checks must be separately implemented and verified.
