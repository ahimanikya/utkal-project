# BharatSHRI: Hathigumpha inscription of Kharavela

Archaeological Survey of India; checked 2026-10-01.

Locator: BRAHMI INSCRIPTION OF KHARAVELA FROM HATHIGUMPHA; reference EI XX, pp. 71–89.

Institutional epigraph catalogue describes Prakrit/Brahmi royal account through regnal year 13 and a salutation to Jinas. Catalogue dates it to first half of second century BCE; another government page uses first century BCE. Jajpur provenance in this catalogue is withheld as conflicting with the Udayagiri/Bhubaneswar site identity. Royal military/welfare claims are not independent measurements.
