# Excavations at Udayagiri-2 (1997–2000), Memoirs of the Archaeological Survey of India No.100

Bimal Bandyopadhyay, ASI, New Delhi, 2007. [Original report](https://asi.nic.in/admin/publications/ePublicationReportsDetails/download/47). Inscription readings credited to **Snigdha Tripathy**; listing credited to G.L. Katade. Relevant pages read in the original PDF; selected inscriptions, chronology and debate pages checked visually.

This is primary excavation evidence for Udayagiri-2, including the Simhaprastha name and occupation interpretation. Its Langudi chapter reproduces Mukherjee’s proposed readings and Pradhan’s identification, then records Bandyopadhyay’s reservations after a 3 December 1999 visit. It is not independent corroboration of the quoted Langudi readings.

The fragmentary lettering and palaeographic dates retain uncertainty. The author explicitly describes limits to stratigraphic separation (p.41). Do not present hypothesised royal patronage or reasons for decline as established events.
