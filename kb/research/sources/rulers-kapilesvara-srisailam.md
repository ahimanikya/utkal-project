# Srisailam Inscription of Kapilesvara, Saka 1382

D. C. Sircar; Archaeological Survey of India, Epigraphia Indica XXXVI (1965–1966), published 1970; digitised by IGNCA; checked 2026-10-01.

Locator: Title page PDF p. 6; article no. 10, printed pp. 75–78 = PDF pp. 124–127; article continues to p. 80.

Inspected the introduction and interpretation on pp. 75–78, not the full volume or all transcription pages. Srisailam slab includes Odia and Telugu-character portions; editor equates Saka 1382 with 3 July 1460. Names Gajapati Kapilesvara of Orissa; calls geographical universal-rule epithet hyperbolic. Donation and personal presence are uncertain. Published scholarly edition of inscription; editor interpretations separated from literal royal claims.
