# From a place story to a usable trip guide

This is a proposed editorial template derived from the [Odisha Tourism study](../about/odisha-tourism-study.md), consistent with the existing visitor-index evidence rules. It does not define a working planner or a new approved website requirement.

## Keep three records distinct

| Record | Meaning | Required link |
|---|---|---|
| Place or attraction | The canonical entity and its factual/cultural context | Stable concept ID and sources |
| Visit or experience | A specific opportunity involving a provider, venue or activity | Place ID; willing provider and access evidence when relevant |
| Itinerary | An ordered proposal connecting stops through travel legs | Stop IDs, start/end, mode, timing and operating checks |

A craft tradition is not a bookable workshop. A food-place association is not a serving venue. An editorial region is not a measured travel radius.

## Planning record template

| Field group | What to record |
|---|---|
| Identity and state | Stable itinerary ID; title; author; revision; research outline / reviewed draft / published; actual named reviewer when present |
| Scope | Start and end points; overnight bases; geography; explicit loop or one-way route; transport mode |
| Reader fit | Interests and supported access features; explain who the route may suit and its known constraints |
| Time | Total days and nights, season, time at each stop and each travel leg; distinguish measured, operator-supplied and estimated values |
| Stops | Canonical place IDs, reason to visit, optional alternatives, access rules, booking or permit requirements |
| Travel legs | From/to IDs, mode, distance, estimated time range, source, check date and conditions; avoid straight-line distance as journey distance |
| Food and stays | Named source-linked providers, actual serving/hosting evidence, availability check and official onward link |
| Guides and providers | Separate professional tour-guide profiles from written guides and agencies; record places served, languages, specialisms, published credential source, check date and public professional contact link. A directory listing does not establish current availability |
| Practical services | Toilets, drinking water, rest areas, parking and access features with confirmed / unknown / unavailable states |
| Costs | Currency, per-person/per-room/per-vehicle basis, inclusions, date and variability; unknown stays blank rather than zero |
| Seasonal changes | Closures, event year, permit windows, weather-sensitive activities and alternative plan |
| Evidence and rights | Per-field source and source-check date, editorial-review date, media credit/rights, conflicts and correction history |

Do not compute a trip budget, duration or route score when required inputs are missing. Suppress a recommendation when a material access conflict remains. Link to the responsible publisher for current booking rather than implying our research record guarantees availability.

## Quality checks before recommending a route

Each stop resolves to the intended place. Every leg has its own travel evidence and sensible allowances. Practical dates are visible. Neighbouring-place text agrees with the page identity. Meals, breaks and overnight locations fit the plan. Official editorial facts, operator offers and contributor accounts are labelled separately. A second editor or knowledgeable local reviewer checks the assembled route and is credited only after doing so.
