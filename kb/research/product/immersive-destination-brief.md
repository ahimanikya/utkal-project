# Connected destination pages and immersive journeys

Founder direction: subpages for sub-locations, places to stay and things to do, each with photographs; responsive layouts that make good use of large screens; connected visual elements and an imagined immersive film with light nature music.

## Delivered candidate

The first 15 illustrated detail pages comprise four places (Mangalajodi, Kalijai, Satapada, Chandrabhaga), six experiences and five stay-area guides. Canonical records live in [details.json](../destinations/details.json). Stable existing experience/base IDs remain; parent links, related places/experiences/stays and evidence connect the hierarchy. Stay areas remain distinct from individual properties. Their photographs depict settings, not rooms. Property pages require verified provider details and appropriately licensed room/property images before being represented as such.

Destination and detail pages use a canvas up to 1,920 CSS pixels, fluid gutters, large photographs and multi-column content on wider displays. Paragraphs retain a readable measure. Small-screen layouts stack. Illustrated connections are reading paths, not maps or claims of journey feasibility.

Each destination has a four-chapter photographic journey: manual play/pause, previous/next, optional full screen, sound off initially, an original quiet pentatonic pad with filtered noise, and no location-recording claim. Playback ends on the final chapter, pauses when the tab is hidden, and audio stops when paused or navigating chapters. Reduced-motion preference disables the slow zoom. The opening scene and chapter link remain usable without JavaScript. It is explicitly labelled as a photo sequence, not a 360° film. No video-generation tool is connected in this session, and no AI film has been generated.

## AI film production brief · next media step

Target: a gentle 30–45 second imagination film for each destination, with an optional ambient instrumental track and visible “AI-imagined interpretation” labelling. No speech is required for the first version; short accessible captions explain the mood and link back to factual pages. Avoid recreating identifiable people or implying a documentary visit.

| Sequence | Chilika concept | Konark concept |
|---|---|---|
| 0–8 seconds | Wide water and a slow boat journey; caption “Choose a shore. Take your time.” | Warm light moving across a carved wheel; caption “Look closer at the stone.” |
| 8–18 seconds | Wetland birds seen at a respectful distance; no improbable flock behaviour or invented species claims | Stone detail gives way to the coast; do not imply a geographically continuous camera move |
| 18–28 seconds | A transition to the island-shrine story, anchored in reference images rather than invented architecture | A performance-inspired visual; label any imagined venue and do not pass off Delhi footage as Konark |
| 28–40 seconds | Food and a return to the lagoon horizon; an invitation to explore the linked places | A regional food detail and return to the monument; an invitation to look for one small carving |

Music direction: quiet, spacious instrumental tones, soft water/wind texture, no dramatic trailer crescendo or sampled copyrighted song. Any commissioned or generated track needs its own rights record. The current browser soundscape is an original prototype, not an authentic folk composition or a field recording.

Production inputs: reference selection, valid reuse/derivative rights, prompts and model/version, generation date, shot log, sound rights and review. Check landmark geometry, costume and ecological plausibility. Export a web-friendly conventional film with poster, captions/transcript, pause/mute and no autoplay sound. Keep source records in the KB.

A genuine 360° experience requires a separate panoramic/equirectangular production path, verified orientation and a compatible viewer. A simulated camera movement or enlarged ordinary photograph must not be labelled 360°. Select a video-generation service and confirm any cost before producing the AI film; no paid account or spend is authorized by this candidate.
