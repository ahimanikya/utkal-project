# City, craft and coast

Founder direction: “go”, approving the proposed five-part coastal collection. Ahimanikya Satapathy remains Founder & Editor-in-Chief and final human authority. This is an implemented local candidate, not an approved publication.

## The visitor experience

Puri now introduces the town’s connected traditions, shore and craft before presenting saved choices. The existing Raghurajpur page pairs an actual artisan photograph with a historical revival account, a dated visitor account and practical questions for a workshop visit. The artisan’s name is not provided by the photograph’s source; no identity, quotation or personal biography is invented.

A new coastal food guide distinguishes Mahaprasad’s devotional context from machha besara, chingudi tarkari and chhena poda. Each retains its existing saved identity. Dietary and preparation questions invite direct confirmation without asserting a restaurant’s suitability.

The new stay comparison gives Puri, Konark and Ramachandi/coastal-road searches the same fields: reason to consider, transport to plan and questions to ask. Quietness, room quality and current access remain unverified. Existing Puri and Konark stay pages remain available and link into this comparison.

“City, craft and coast” creates a separate eight-idea journey spanning Bhubaneswar, Dhauli, Konark, Puri and Raghurajpur, plus two food ideas and a base. Everything starts unscheduled. It is not a driving route or a promised duration. Existing starters and saved plans remain intact.

## Source trail

- [Orissa Review, December 2005](https://magazines.odisha.gov.in/Orissareview/dec2005/engpdf/raghurajpur_crafts_village_extraordinary.pdf): historical craft account, including its citation of Ileana Citaristi about renewed opportunities around 1953 and Halina Zealey. Used with explicit attribution; no old transport or household figures adopted.
- [Biswa Prakash’s 2019 visit, Odisha Tourism](https://apps.odishatourism.gov.in/blog-detail/raghurajpur-the-heritage-crafts-village-of-odisha-odishabloggerstrail): a visitor’s report of making and explanation, not a promise that every house welcomes visitors.
- [Incredible India: Puri](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/odisha/puri): food descriptions and seasonal starting point, without claiming inspected kitchens or live weather.
- [Odisha Tourism: food](https://odishatourism.gov.in/content/tourism/en/the-taste-of-odisha.html): Mahaprasad context. No unsupported health claims adopted.
- [Incredible India: Ramachandi paragraph](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/odisha/bhubaneswar/experience-the-natural-beauty-of-bhubaneswar): river-and-sea setting only. Broader safety, accessibility and unrelated claims omitted.
- [Rangan Datta Wiki’s Raghurajpur photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Raghurajpur_1.jpg): 28 August 2018; CC BY-SA 4.0. Source description and full image inspected; resized to 1600 × 1059, no generated elements. Source URL, original URL, hash, credit and licence are in `../destinations/regions.json`. Credit is carried into the website and illustrated journey catalogue.

Existing Puri, Raghurajpur and food citations remain in their canonical packets. The historical account and the photographed artisan are not represented as the same personal story. No interviews, partnerships, booking arrangements or outreach were performed.

## Implementation and verification

The two regional food guides now share `RegionalMeal.astro`, preserving the Bhubaneswar guide’s content and save IDs. The generic detail page supports short section-menu labels independently of full headings. The portrait/full-frame instruction preserves the artisan’s context. All are UTP adapters over Design System rc.4; no new shared-package version.

The comparison uses existing `stay:puri`; the earlier `base:konark-puri` and `base:konark-local` identities are retained on their existing pages. Konark secondary food choices now render their detail links. Raghurajpur’s Kotpad connection was merged into its one onward section, rather than lost with the duplicate ending.

72 pages built; 78 site tests and 6 design-system tests passed. Twenty-eight responsive checks across 360, 768, 1440 and 1920 pixels found no horizontal page overflow. Targeted desktop/phone visual inspection and actual browser journey creation/edit/reload were completed. The older six-idea Bhubaneswar plan retained its note and assigned day.
