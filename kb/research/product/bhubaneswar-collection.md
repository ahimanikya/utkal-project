# Bhubaneswar: a connected collection

Founder direction: “go”, approving the proposed Mukteswar, food, stay-area, connection and responsive-review batch. This implements that scope locally; it is not publication approval. Ahimanikya Satapathy retains final editorial authority.

## What a reader can do

- Enter Mukteswar through the complete gateway photograph, then follow architecture, respectful visiting and an onward choice.
- Explore dalma, pakhala and chhena poda through a city meal guide, with separate dish saves and links to the longer food stories.
- Compare an Old Town/Ekamra search, Janpath/central-city search and an arrival-focused base through consistent fields: reason to consider, transport to plan, questions before booking.
- Follow all three from the city, discovery, planning collections, correction entry selector and the six-idea “Stone and painted streets” starter.
- Create a separate unscheduled journey, rename it, assign days and keep notes. Existing three-item city journeys retain their saved choices, dates and notes.

## Canonical model and presentation

Mukteswar and the stay guide live in `../destinations/central-details.json`; the meal guide lives in `../food/bhubaneswar.json`. The generic detail renderer accepts optional area-comparison options within a section and a single-ending flag. Full-frame portrait treatment is scoped to detail covers using an existing asset fit instruction. These are website adapters over Design System rc.4, not a new package release.

Save IDs remain `place:mukteswar`, `stay:bhubaneswar`, `food:bhubaneswar-dalma`, `food:pakhala` and the pre-existing `food:konark-chhena-poda`. No migration or duplicate food identity was introduced. Old city anchors remain available. Only newly created starters acquire the expanded six-item selection.

## Evidence and interpretation

[Odisha Tourism: Mukteswar](https://odishatourism.gov.in/content/tourism/en/discover/attractions/temples-monuments/mukteswar-temple.html) supports the tenth-century date, Shiva dedication, torana and described carving. Unverified event dates and broad social interpretations were omitted.

[Odisha Tourism: City life](https://apps.odishatourism.gov.in/city-life) supports the broad Janpath/old-city distinction. The comparison is editorial planning advice, with no hotel ranking, current pricing, travel-time estimate or inspected accessibility claim.

[Odisha Tourism: Taste of Odisha](https://odishatourism.gov.in/content/tourism/en/the-taste-of-odisha.html) supports the basic dalma and pakhala descriptions. Unsupported health claims on that source were not adopted. [The chef-authored chhena poda account](https://apps.odishatourism.gov.in/blog-detail/chhenapoda) remains linked through the existing dish research. Tasting questions are original prompts, not quotations from a cook.

Existing credited photographs are reused. The stay cover explicitly shows heritage context, not accommodation. The food cover identifies its Puri photograph rather than implying a photographed Bhubaneswar restaurant. Credits remain in accessible end disclosures.
