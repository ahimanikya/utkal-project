# Biswanath Das — Odisha statesman

Biswanath Das provides a concrete route into Odisha’s participation in Constitution-making. CLPR identifies him as an Orissa representative in the Constituent Assembly. Its transcription of the **20 August 1949** proceedings records his objections to draft Article 277, especially the risks of emergency powers affecting provincial financial allocations.
