# Anangabhima Deva III

**Anangabhima Deva III connects Utkal’s royal history with Cuttack as a centre of government.** Sarita Nayak’s April 2021 Odisha Review article discusses the Nagari plates, Varanasi-Kataka and royal charters issued from Cuttack. It also reports a Purusottama installation in 1230–31 CE.

The presently captured evidence is the indexed text of a contributed historical article, not our own reading of the original copper plates. The same article distinguishes charter evidence from the Madalapanji narrative. Those traditions should not be collapsed into a claim that one ruler alone founded Cuttack or every surviving feature of Barabati Fort.

The existing project Barabati destination record supplies architectural context; it remains a separate place record. Next research should compare the charter editions and archaeological construction phases. This profile is a sourced research nucleus with original-charter verification pending.

## Jagannath evidence checkpoint · 2 October 2026 UTC

Sircar’s original epigraphic edition documents four Puri-complex inscriptions from Saka 1147 and 1158. Its second record uses a sovereignty formula subordinating the ruler to Purusottama. Calendar disagreements in two records and alternative accession interpretations remain explicit. Do not transfer the separate Cuttack installation date to Puri.
