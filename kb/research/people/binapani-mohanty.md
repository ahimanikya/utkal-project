# Binapani Mohanty

Binapani Mohanty combined economics teaching with Odia fiction. Sahitya Akademi’s indexed 2014 profile connects her childhood to Chandol in Kendrapara and records retirement from S. B. Women’s College in 1992. The Akademi profile and AU Press both record the **1990 Sahitya Akademi award for the Pata Dei story collection**. The collection and its title story are separate bibliographic objects.

Our reading entry is the title story’s 2016 English version by Ashok K. Mohanty. The Akademi profile also describes an earlier translation by Jayanta Mahapatra, published as Lata in Femina in 1986; the magazine issue has not been inspected.

**Conflict held:** the Akademi excerpt gives the state award year as 1968; AU Press gives 1970. Neither is selected for a public chronology. The 2016 biography’s office-holding language is not presented as current.
