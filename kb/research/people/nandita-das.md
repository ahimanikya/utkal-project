# Nandita Das

Nandita Das connects Odisha with an international film career through both family heritage and the stories she chooses to tell. In her own 2013 essay she describes her father’s Baripada roots and her childhood visits there. She also states that she was born in Bombay and raised in Delhi; this is a heritage connection, not an Odisha birthplace claim.

[Manto](../works/manto-film.md), which she directed and wrote, appears in Cannes’ 2018 Un Certain Regard selection. [Zwigato](../works/zwigato-film.md) takes viewers into the working life of a delivery rider’s family in Bhubaneswar. Its festival year is 2022, while her official profile dates theatrical release to 17 March 2023.
