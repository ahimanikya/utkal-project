# Narasimha Deva I

**Narasimha Deva I offers one of the strongest connections between royal power and a surviving Odisha landmark: Konark.** UNESCO places the Sun Temple in his reign, **AD 1238–1264**, and interprets its architecture as testimony to the strength and stability of the Ganga Empire.

[Konark Sun Temple](../places/konark.md) connects this ruler to the existing monument record. Royal patronage and the labour of architects, sculptors and other makers are distinct contributions; naming the ruler does not assign him personal authorship of the sculpture.

UNESCO recognition belongs to the site. It is evidence of the monument’s international significance, not a ranking of the king’s popularity or an economic estimate for his reign. Precise campaign histories and competing reign-end dates should be checked in epigraphic editions before extending the timeline.
