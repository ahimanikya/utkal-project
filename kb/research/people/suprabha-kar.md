# Suprabha Kar

The 2016 AU Press contributor note identifies Suprabha Kar as a teacher at Ravenshaw Girls’ School in Cuttack and an advocate of women’s education. It records her regular contributions to Utkal Sahitya. Birth and death dates are explicitly unknown in that source.

Her story **The Vigil** appears in English with credits to Arundhati Patnaik and Priyadarshi Patnaik. An indexed library contents record pairs Pratikhya with The vigil; the original publication date and matching original text still need verification.
