# Sanjukta Panigrahi

The Akademi’s joint citation credits Sanjukta Panigrahi with Odissi dance, teaching and choreography. It records earlier Kalakshetra training and later study with Kelucharan Mohapatra. Its dance register dates the joint recognition with Raghunath Panigrahi to 1976.

Both records were available as indexed institutional text; direct retrieval failed. A named, dated performance recording and its full credits remain to be established.
