# Anantavarman Chodaganga Deva

**Anantavarman Chodaganga Deva’s clearest public-facing connection is the Jagannath Temple at Puri.** The Government of Odisha’s historical overview attributes the present temple to this Ganga ruler in the twelfth century.

This provides a strong link between royal patronage and one of Odisha’s central religious institutions. It does not make the king the temple’s architect or the sole builder of every part of the complex. Construction phases, later additions and specific grants need their own records.

[Jagannath Temple, Puri](../places/jagannath-temple-puri.md) connects royal patronage with the existing place record. A fuller political biography should establish his territorial changes and dates from charters before assigning numbers to his power.
