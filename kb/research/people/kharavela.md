# Kharavela

**Kharavela brings ancient Kalinga into focus through a surviving royal inscription.** ASI’s Hathigumpha catalogue describes an account of his education, accession, welfare activity and campaigns, continuing through his thirteenth regnal year. Its opening salutation to the Jinas and Odisha Tourism’s cave interpretation connect his memory with Jain heritage at Udayagiri and Khandagiri.

The inscription is a royal account. Its campaign and welfare claims provide historical evidence to examine, not a modern popularity poll. ASI’s catalogue uses a second-century BCE date, while the Tourism architecture overview uses the first century BCE. This profile therefore keeps the precise chronology open. The catalogue’s Jajpur location is also withheld because it conflicts with the Bhubaneswar cave-site identification.

Read the [ASI catalogue](https://bharatshri.asi.gov.in/gallery?lang=en), then pursue the original Epigraphia Indica XX edition before adding battle itineraries, irrigation measurements or a territorial map.
