# Sujata Mohapatra

Sujata Mohapatra brings Odissi into a contemporary performing career. Her official biography identifies Balasore as her place of origin; that particular capture is indexed evidence because the direct page could not be retrieved.

The Ministry of Culture lists her for the Sangeet Natak Akademi Award for **2017** in Odissi. Award year, selection and ceremony timing differ: the announcement reports selection in June 2018 and a ceremony planned for February 2019. A later PIB report identifies her as a recipient and documents her [JIPMER recital on 2 November 2025](../works/sujata-jipmer-recital-2025.md).
