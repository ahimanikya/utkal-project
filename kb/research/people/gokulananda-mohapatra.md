# Gokulananda Mohapatra

**1922–2013.** A science writer and chemistry educator associated with Kuansa near Bhadrak, Mohapatra brought scientific subjects into Odia reading and broadcasting. His contribution connects science education with literature: [E Jugara Shrestha Abiskar](../works/e-jugara-shrestha-abiskar.md) appears in the state’s award register, while [Baigyanika Gyanakosha](../works/baigyanika-gyanakosha.md) is an encyclopaedic reference-work lead.

The evidence supports a story about making science accessible in Odia. It does not yet quantify readership, learning outcomes, sales or exports. Mohapatra/Mahapatra are name variants for this person; Gokulananda Das and Gokulananda Mohanty are not automatically the same identity.
