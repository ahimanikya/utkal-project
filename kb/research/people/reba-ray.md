# Reba Ray

Reba Ray worked across education, editing and Odia literature. AU Press’s 2016 contributor note identifies her as the founder of Asha and records Sanyasi in Utkal Sahitya, volume three, in 1899. The anthology’s editors describe it as the first published Odia short story by a woman; this is an attributed historical assessment, not an independently checked exhaustive claim.

Her story is available in English as **The Mendicant**, translated by K. K. Mohapatra.

**Date conflict:** AU Press gives 1875–1957; the indexed Indiana University catalogue gives 1876–1957. Birth year remains unresolved. Death year 1957 agrees in these records.
