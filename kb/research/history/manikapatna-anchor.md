# A stone anchor connects Odisha and Gujarat

Tripati and colleagues analysed a broken medieval-type anchor found at Manikapatna using petrography, chemistry and isotopes. They identified Palitana-area Deccan basalt in Gujarat as its most likely rock source.[^maritime-anchor]

## Why it matters

This is a material-provenance connection, stronger than a resemblance-based cultural claim. It supports investigating movement across the Indian coastal network. The finding does not identify a ship, crew, itinerary or traded cargo quantity.

## Marketing treatment
