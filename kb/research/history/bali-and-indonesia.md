# Bali and Indonesia: three distinct kinds of connection

Ardika and Bellwood’s Sembiran research identifies early Indian contact with Bali.[^maritime-sembiran] Odisha’s official Bali Jatra account commemorates maritime exchange with Southeast Asia, particularly Bali.[^maritime-culture-2025]

## Link carefully

The first supports an archaeological India–Bali connection; the second establishes a contemporary Odisha commemoration. Neither alone proves that every Indian object in Bali came from modern Odisha. The research graph keeps both links without collapsing them into an exclusive origin claim.

## An epigraphic lead

A scholarly discussion references foreign communities including Kling in Javanese inscriptions. This edition records it as a lead: the underlying transcription, dating and geographic interpretation must be checked. The term must not be automatically equated with modern Odia identity.[^maritime-cane-lead]

## Commercial next step

Obtain Odisha-origin exports to Indonesia by product and year, then identify an actual buyer or producer relationship. National India–Indonesia totals cannot fill a state-level data gap.
