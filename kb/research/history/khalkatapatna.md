# Khalkatapatna: a medieval international connection

The 2015 research note associates Khalkatapatna with thirteenth–fifteenth-century contacts with China and Arabia. Its field observations include ceramics and ring wells exposed by riverbank erosion.[^maritime-khalkatapatna]

## Meaning

Use the site as evidence of a trading landscape, not as a proxy for an ancient national trade total. “Arabia” is a historical regional attribution; do not silently convert it into the modern UAE, Saudi Arabia or another single country.

## Next evidence

Obtain the excavation catalogue, specimen identifiers, ceramic provenance and date ranges. The published note’s wording varies between sections on the precise activity window; keep a broad medieval description until harmonised.
