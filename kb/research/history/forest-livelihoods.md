# Forest livelihoods: a documented history

**Use dated evidence to explain how collection, household knowledge and trading institutions changed.** A publication date is not the beginning of a tradition.

- **1973:** OFDC's institutional history places kendu nationalisation in this year. Its page also describes later deregulation in named areas. This is a historical anchor, not a statement of today's collection permissions. [OFDC](https://www.odishafdc.com/kenduleaf.php).
- **2001:** Nanda and Sutar's Vasundhara study, hosted by FAO, connects mahua and kendu livelihoods with community forest management and fire. Its cases need local attribution; the title/abstract district mismatch remains unresolved. This does not establish one uniform tribal practice. [Original study](https://www.fao.org/4/ad352t/AD352T06.htm).
