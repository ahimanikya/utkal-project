# Palur and reconstructed Bay of Bengal routes

## Evidence type

These are published historical reconstructions combining texts and archaeology. Represent them as interpreted routes, with source attribution and approximate periods. Do not draw them as measured vessel tracks.

Ports named together in a network may lie in different present-day states. A network link can be commercially meaningful without being exclusive to one region.
