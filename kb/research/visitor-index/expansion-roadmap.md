# Growing the Odisha discovery indexes

Build positive impressions through specific things a reader can discover, experience, buy or share. Start with useful linked collections; introduce comparison scores only when their method and coverage justify them.

| Sequence | Index | Reader benefit | Evidence to collect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Now | Food + heritage + nearby services | Turn curiosity into a possible visit | Place links, named providers, access, routes, services |
| Next | Woven & made | Discover skills and buy from credited makers | Maker identity, technique, workshop access, buying route |
| Next | Nature & weekends | Find a reason to return | Seasonal access, permits, transport, hosts |
| Then | Festivals & living culture | Plan around shared experiences | Organiser-confirmed dates, participation rules, local voices |
| Then | Family & accessible visits | Match a trip to actual needs | Feature-level access checks, toilets, rest areas, route conditions |
| Later | Active Odisha | Discover sports, movement and wellness experiences | Current venues, instructors, trails and practical access |
| Later | Enterprise & growth | Connect pride with trade possibilities | Comparable multi-year outcomes, realised facilities, credited businesses |

## Geographic expansion

Deepen the five seed areas, then extend to Koraput, southern Odisha, western Odisha and additional northern/coastal destinations. Use the existing place, food and handloom records as research starting points. Do not interpret the first collection’s coastal/city concentration as a ranking of regions.

## Useful measures

Track complete visitor pages, destinations with linked food providers, credited makers, operational details checked within their review window and reader actions such as saved trips or enquiries. These measure this project’s coverage and response. They do not establish statewide tourism quality, cleanliness, economic impact or happiness.

## Public experience

**Explore → Taste → Plan your visit → Meet the makers → Discover more.**

Lead with an inviting detail and a credited image. Let the reader expand sources and practical information. Show a specific next action, such as checking the official museum page or contacting a featured maker, once that route is established.
