# Langudi Hill and the Pushpagiri identification

**Langudi is a distinct Buddhist archaeological site in Jajpur.** Its proposed identification with Xuanzang’s Pushpagiri belongs to the history of scholarship as well as to modern interpretation.

The ASI Udayagiri report summarises B.N. Mukherjee’s readings of fragmentary Langudi inscriptions and D.R. Pradhan’s identification. The same author, Bimal Bandyopadhyay, records reservations after his 1999 visit and calls for further examination. [ASI 2007, pp.13–17](https://asi.nic.in/admin/publications/ePublicationReportsDetails/download/47). The saved Jajpur exhibition presents the identification affirmatively; retain that attribution rather than claiming universal agreement.

This checkpoint has not inspected the original 1997/1998 Langudi inscription articles, their full plates or all later research. It does not settle the site’s foundation date or establish a single university incorporating Ratnagiri, Lalitgiri and Udayagiri. Current access, facilities and media permissions remain unverified.
