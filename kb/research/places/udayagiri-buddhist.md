## Two named monastic establishments

The ASI report distinguishes **Madhavapura Mahavihara** in the earlier excavated area from **Simhaprastha Mahavihara** at Udayagiri-2. Its inscriptions chapter includes accession-level readings; the report broadly places Udayagiri-2’s remains in c. eighth–twelfth centuries CE. These are archaeological names and interpretations, not proof of a single Pushpagiri university. [ASI 2007,pp.1,41,123,141](https://asi.nic.in/admin/publications/ePublicationReportsDetails/download/47).
