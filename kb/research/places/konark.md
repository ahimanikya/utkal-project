# Konark Sun Temple

## Sourced knowledge

Konark’s Sun Temple, in Odisha’s Puri district, was built in the 13th century under Narasimha Deva I. Its architecture takes the form of the sun god Surya’s chariot, with carved wheels and horses. UNESCO inscribed the temple on the World Heritage List in 1984.[^konark]
