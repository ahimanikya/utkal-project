# Chilika

## Sourced knowledge

Chilika is a brackish-water lagoon on Odisha’s coast, where freshwater and marine environments meet. The Chilika Development Authority describes it as a habitat for migratory birds and a source of livelihoods for fishing communities.[^cda]

Chilika Lake was designated a Wetland of International Importance under the Ramsar Convention on 1 October 1981.[^ramsar]
