# Ekamra Kanan: botanical collections and plant research

**The distinction is the cactus collection.** RPRC’s [Cacti page](https://rprcbbsr.in/researchanddevelopment/conservation/cacti) reports **1,050 cactus species and varieties** and describes this as Asia’s largest cactus collection. That is an attributed institutional claim about a collection; it does not establish that Ekamra Kanan is Asia’s biggest park.

The same page reports approximately **200 cactus varieties/hybrids developed** through its research. Species, varieties and hybrids are different categories. The collection and development counts must not be added together or presented as 1,250 species. Their inventory dates are not supplied.

## A garden that became a research institution

RPRC’s [history](https://www.rprcbbsr.in/about-us/history) dates its establishment to **1985**, initially as a recreational garden and green space for Bhubaneswar. It describes a subsequent research role spanning plant conservation and resource use. Ekamra Kanan is its botanical garden in Nayapalli; it is separate from the State Botanical Garden adjoining Nandankanan.
