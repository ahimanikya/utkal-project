# Woven in Odisha: meet the making

**Campaign line:** “Know the hands. Know the cloth.”

Begin with a single piece rather than a sweeping claim. A [Kotpad](../handlooms/kotpad.md) textile, a [Khandua](../handlooms/khandua.md) piece or a [Habaspuri](../handlooms/habaspuri.md) example can each lead to a distinct portrait. Ask the maker to explain the decisions visible in the finished cloth.

## Proposed feature structure

Show the whole piece, then a detail. Name the maker and making place. Explain the technique only after it has been verified for that piece. Include material, care and a transparent purchase route where one is established. Record the source of all sustainability claims separately.

For fashion audiences, style a credited object in contemporary use. For design audiences, document process and material choices. Do not describe every Odisha textile as Sambalpuri or every patterned textile as ikat.

## Commercial research

Interview participating makers about commissions, capacity, payment terms and how they want to be represented. Check a seller’s product provenance and current fulfilment. A story should help readers understand value without inventing income, labour-time or market-demand statistics.
