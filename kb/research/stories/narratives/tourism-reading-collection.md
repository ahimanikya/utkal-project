# Six stories to read before a visit

These articles develop six existing Odisha place and craft records into connected reading. The Founder explicitly approved merging this collection into the project repository on 6 October 2026. The articles retain editorial draft status until prepared for website publication. The [Founder TODO](../../../specs/founder-todo.md) holds deferred field-pilot decisions; article preparation can proceed while those decisions wait.

| Article | Reader discovery | Useful next evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Kala Bhoomi and the art of looking closely](kala-bhoomi-looking-closely.md) | Household objects and woven mathematics can change how we look at craft. | Current object labels, maker credits and permitted object photography. |
| [Dhauli and the instructions to power](dhauli-instructions-to-power.md) | A celebrated sentence speaks to officials about their treatment of people. | Specialist reading of the inscription and current approach conditions. |
| [Pipili appliqué from ceremony to everyday life](pipili-cloth-and-ceremony.md) | A cut edge or machine stitch can open a conversation about skill. | A willing maker’s own account, object provenance and workshop conditions. |
| [Cuttack Tarakasi and the structure inside silver](cuttack-tarakasi-structure-in-silver.md) | Thicker frames and finer wire make a delicate pattern possible. | A named maker’s account and material documentation. |
| [Jobra and the workshop behind the Maritime Museum](jobra-workshop-maritime-museum.md) | Boats and waterways depend on workshops, tools and people. | Original plaque, workers’ histories and permitted machine photographs. |
| [Raghurajpur where painting meets performance](raghurajpur-painting-and-performance.md) | A painter’s family and a dancer’s training open two ways to read a story. | Artist interpretation, performance arrangements and local voices. |

[First-batch claim and media record](tourism-reading-evidence.json) separates new source inspection, reused evidence, original narration and remaining questions. Existing place records and photographs remain canonical; no duplicate place identities or invented interviews were created.

The [second-batch claim and media record](tourism-reading-followup-evidence.json) covers Tarakasi, Jobra and Raghurajpur. The six drafts complete the first reading collection for the [six-experience pack](../../../specs/tourism-fieldwork/index.md).
