# Two Bhubaneswar universities in India’s top 20

**Two Bhubaneswar institutions appear in the top 20 of NIRF’s 2025 University category:** SOA at 15 and KIIT at 17. This is a dated, category-specific national comparison.

| Institution | 2024 rank / score | 2025 rank / score |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SOA | 14 /62.61 | 15 /63.14 |
| KIIT | 15 /62.53 | 17 /62.87 |

Both published scores rose slightly while rank positions slipped. Keep both facts visible. Ranking pools and methods can change; the differences do not prove a measured rise in teaching quality or graduate earnings. [2024 table](https://www.nirfindia.org/Rankings/2024/UniversityRanking.html) · [2025 table](https://www.nirfindia.org/Rankings/2025/UniversityRanking.html).

**Beyond rank: follow the graduate cohort.** In their 2025 submissions, the 2023–24 four-year undergraduate cohorts report median annual pay among placed graduates of ₹6 lakh at SOA and ₹9.7 lakh at KIIT. These are institution-reported aggregates across four-year programmes, not engineering-only salaries, all-graduate earnings or promises to applicants.
