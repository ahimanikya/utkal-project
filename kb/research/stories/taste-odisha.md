# Taste Odisha: a collection of kitchens

**Campaign line:** “One state. So many ways to welcome you.”

Open with the act of serving. Move between [pakhala](../food/pakhala.md), [dalma](../food/dalma.md), the [pitha collection](../food/pitha.md) and a [chhena poda](../food/chhena-poda.md) slice. Let each contributor explain what the food means in their own home or business. These selected examples introduce a collection; they do not stand in for every community’s cuisine.

## Proposed formats

- Poster: one dish, its name, place or contributor, and one sensory line grounded in the photographed preparation.
- Short feature: one maker, one attributed preparation, one variation and one occasion.
- Video: serving close-up, maker’s voice, process detail, finished plate and place name.

For a national audience, test “your next food journey.” For international audiences, retain the local name and add a plain-language ingredient or technique description. Gather audience response before choosing a universal message.
