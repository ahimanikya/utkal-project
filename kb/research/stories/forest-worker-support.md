# Forest work: understanding the support behind the trade

**₹24.635 crore in compassionate grants is recorded for FY2025–26** on Odisha's Kendu Leaf Organisation welfare page. The accompanying total is **1,235 grant records** across pluckers/binders and seasonal staff. [Official table](https://kenduleaves.odisha.gov.in/welfare-measures).

This is one specific form of support associated with death or permanent disability. It is not the whole welfare budget, the trade's sales value or the earnings of a typical collector. The page was read on 1 October 2026 and displays a 9 September update; individual payments have not been independently audited here.

| Follow the money | What the measure tells us |
| --- | --- |
| Sale proceeds | Value of marketed leaves |
| Wages and collection payments | Payment for work or leaf supply |
| Bonuses and incentives | Separate programme benefits |
| Compassionate grants | Support under a specific welfare category |

These rows are categories to distinguish, not an additive flow diagram. A larger compassionate-grant amount does not by itself show improved wellbeing.
