# Growing Odisha: numbers with a human setting

**Campaign line:** “See what is taking shape.”

The [GDP story](../economy/gdp-story.md) introduces scale. Individual features can then explain a port, an industrial activity, fisheries or a service business through an attributed participant and a relevant measure.

Suggested first stories:

- [Fish production](../economy/comparison-01-fisheries.md): a ten-year physical-output comparison, paired with a producer-led account. Do not convert production growth into an unmeasured income claim.
- [Paradip throughput](../economy/comparison-05-port-logistics.md): a port logistics story with the period clearly visible. Do not infer the same growth for all Odisha trade.
- [Construction real value added](../economy/construction.md): a five-year sector chart, with local examples researched separately. Do not present all output as government infrastructure.

Use one measure per poster, the full period beside it and a legible source note. A short video can put the number on screen once and spend its remaining time explaining the activity behind it.

Proposed investment records should form a separate “what is planned” sequence. An approved project, a groundbreaking and commercial production are different milestones.
