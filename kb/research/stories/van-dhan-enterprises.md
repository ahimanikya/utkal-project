# Forest products, local enterprise: a documented Odisha strength

**₹10.72 crore in reported Van Dhan sales placed Odisha highest in a December 2023 parliamentary table.** The underlying figure is ₹1,072.01 lakh, covering cumulative programme reporting rather than one year's turnover. [Reply, annexure p.3](https://sansad.in/getFile/loksabhaquestions/annex/1714/AU1179.pdf?source=pqals).

Odisha accounted for about **26.1%** of the published all-India programme total. This includes both forest and non-forest products; it is not a measure of the entire forest economy, exports or collector income.

One way to tell the human story is to follow the work: a leaf becomes a plate, honey is processed, and tamarind is deseeded. A [2021 ministry account](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=1715921) names community clusters pursuing these activities. Their individual sales and current operations remain research questions.

Suggested campaign direction: **“The value of a forest also lives in the work of its people.”** This is editorial wording, not a measured income-impact claim. Keep the December 2023 date visible in any card or poster; this evidence does not support a present-day ranking.
