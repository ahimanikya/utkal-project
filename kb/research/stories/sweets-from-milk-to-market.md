# From milk to market: the work behind a sweet

**A sweet connects more than a shop and a customer.** A published Pahala case study traces chhena from Niali to sweet makers and markets elsewhere in Odisha and West Bengal. It makes dairy supply, preparation and distribution part of the same story. [Study, December 2023, pp.2306–2308](https://ndpublisher.in/admin/issues/EAv68n5z11.pdf).
