# Institution building, measured one stage at a time

**₹25.20 crore in reported university expenses, FY 2024–25.** KISS Deemed to be University’s signed statement gives a concrete financial scale for one part of Odisha’s education landscape.

The prior-year column is ₹24.02 crore: about 4.94% nominal growth. Expenses include depreciation; the figures are neither cash spending nor measured social impact. They cover the university, not the KISS school, Foundation or KIIT. [Statement, p.2](https://university.kiss.ac.in/docs/Accounts/Balance_Sheets/KISSDU_FY_2024-25.pdf).

The original university notification explicitly distinguishes the school. Keep that boundary visible when connecting institutions, money and students. [Notification, p.2](https://university.kiss.ac.in/docs/IIQA/KISS-DU-NOTIFICATION-DT-25.08.2017.pdf).

## A second lens: access to postgraduate study

**948 postgraduate students with institution-funded full tuition reimbursement** are reported in KISS’s 2025 NIRF SDG submission. This covers two-year PG programmes across study years, with no explicit census day on the row. It is institution-reported tuition support, not proof of independently measured learning or employment impact. [Submission, p.1](https://university.kiss.ac.in/docs/NIRF/SDG_Institutions.pdf).
