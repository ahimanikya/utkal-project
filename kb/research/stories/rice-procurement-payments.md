# The money moving through Odisha’s paddy procurement

**₹28,718 crore in MSP and input assistance** was reported paid to paddy farmers for **KMS 2024–25**. That is the Food Supplies & Consumer Welfare Department’s combined payment figure. It is not farmer profit. [Annual report, printed p.29](https://foododisha.in/Download/annual-activity-glance-25-26.pdf).

The same report separates **7,345,707 tonnes in kharif** and **1,918,528 tonnes in rabi** in its detailed table: about **92.64 lakh tonnes across the marketing year**. Seasonal quantities can be added; seasonal farmer counts cannot be treated as unique annual people without removing overlap.

The combined payment is close to, but does not exactly equal, the detailed quantity multiplied by the common-paddy rate plus assistance. Settlement details remain unverified. The earlier year’s figure covers MSP alone, so a payment-growth percentage would mix categories.
