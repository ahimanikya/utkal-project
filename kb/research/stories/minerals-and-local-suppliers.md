# Beyond the metal: Odisha’s small suppliers

**₹652.19 crore in purchases from Odisha-based micro and small enterprises.** That is what NALCO reports for 2025–26, giving the mineral economy a concrete connection to smaller businesses.

The company reports ₹1,447.94 crore in total purchases from MSEs across locations. Odisha-based suppliers therefore account for **about 45% of this MSE total**. This is procurement of goods and services, not supplier profit, jobs created or a share of all company purchasing. [NALCO annual report, printed p.56, section 19(a)](https://nalcoindia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/45th-Annual-Report-2025-26.pdf).
