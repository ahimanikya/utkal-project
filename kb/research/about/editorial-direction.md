# Utkal Project: editorial and visual direction

Current voice and narration follow the [Utkal storytelling standard](../../reference/storytelling-standard.md), directed by the Founder on 29 September 2026 UTC. The earlier naming and palette proposals below are superseded by the settled **Utkal Project** name, **Rediscover Utkal. Reimagine Odisha.** tagline and [brand direction 02](../../reference/brand-direction-02.md). They remain here as proposal history.

## Current campaign priority

Tourism leads the public campaign, supported by food, handlooms, culture and city experiences. The objective is to build pride, curiosity and demand for Odisha, with broader trade and industry chapters later. Follow the [tourism-first strategy](tourism-first-strategy.md); the user’s future personal business is not the organising premise.

## Brand proposition

**Utkala — A living encyclopedia of Odisha**

**A world of stories, waiting to be shared.**

The name and descriptor are the current [identity proposal](brand-identity.md). The second line is optional campaign copy, not a second brand name.

Use Utkala as the user’s chosen cultural expression and Odisha as the clear contemporary geographic name. Keep Odisha in page titles and search descriptions so audiences can find the work. This is a naming strategy, not a claim that historical territorial boundaries were identical in every period.

## Voice

Warm, specific and visually led. Name the food, the textile, the place and the person. Invite curiosity. Build pride through detail. Let the photograph and a short memorable line open the story; put evidence behind the detail.

Suggested recurring invitations: “Taste Odisha.” “Meet its makers.” “Follow a different road.” “See what is growing.” These are creative proposals, not existing official slogans.

## Palette proposal

|---|---|---|
| Deep red | #8F2435 | Identity and main accents |
| Warm ivory | #F5EBDD | Background and breathing room |

| Lagoon teal | #245C59 | Links, secondary actions and selected data series |

This is an art-direction choice for this project. The KB has not established a single official or historically definitive colour representing all Odisha. Keep photographs true to the individual textile, food or place; do not recolour cultural objects to fit the palette.

## India and international framing

For Indian audiences, test weekend discovery, regional food and meaningful purchases as entry points. For global audiences, test making processes, named landscapes and explained cultural context. Avoid treating either audience as uniform: language, motivation and familiarity should guide later testing.

## Copy discipline

Use a strong supported detail instead of an unverified superlative. “More to discover” is an invitation; “never discovered” erases the people who know a place. A growth story needs a period and measure. A maker story needs a name and credit. A travel story needs current practical information before it becomes a recommendation.

## Presenting numbers

Follow the [statistics presentation standard](statistics-storytelling.md): one clear takeaway, one main number, a useful comparison and a simple visual. Explain what the measure means. Keep periods, units and material qualifications visible, with deeper evidence on demand.
