# How knowledge is recorded and reused

## What this collection contains

This bundle follows the published Open Knowledge Format 0.2: concept documents in Markdown with YAML metadata, source references, lifecycle fields and linked indexes.[^okf] The producer’s additional fields describe geography, aliases, evidence status and publication readiness. They are local extensions, not new OKF requirements.

## Three layers

1. **Sourced knowledge:** a narrow factual statement tied to a source, with the source’s scope retained.
2. **Interpretation and creative proposals:** editorial choices, campaign language and suggested connections. These are not measured findings.
3. **Open research:** missing local detail, disagreements, operational checks and questions requiring specialist or participant input.

## Evidence handling

Primary registries support designation claims. Statistical publications support named measures and periods. Institutional tourism pages support destination and cultural descriptions, but promotional assertions still require judgment. A contributor post on a government-hosted site remains a contributor post; a chef interview describes that chef’s account, not every household’s practice.

New cultural records were checked against targeted web page or PDF extracts. Their verification scope is stated in metadata. Some source access consisted of indexed passages, not complete PDFs. Source entries disclose this; the collection does not pretend to contain complete archived originals. Only concise extracted knowledge and bibliographic information are retained.

Earlier economic and city evidence was migrated with its original caveats. It is marked as imported and has no new source-verification event. Recalculating a percentage establishes arithmetic consistency, not source accuracy. No record claims human review.

## Freshness

Review dates are editorial schedules. They do not mean a historical designation expires on that date. Recheck financial releases after the next survey; live travel information before each publication; registry validity before a current GI claim; and historical/cultural records annually or when new evidence appears.

## Reuse

Search locally first. Follow the source and caveat for a proposed claim. If the record answers a stable historical question, reuse the saved nucleus. Browse again when the question is new, the record is stale, sources conflict or a current operational detail is needed. Add the result here so the same research is not repeated.

This edition is a substantial starting collection. It is not a census of Odisha’s foods, communities, textiles or destinations.
