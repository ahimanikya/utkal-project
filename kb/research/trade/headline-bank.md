## Hold or rewrite

“Odisha created Southeast Asian civilisation” has no defensible basis in this collection. “Continuous world trade for 2,000 years” is not established by dated finds. “Paradip’s cargo is all Odisha exports” misstates coverage. “Bali Jatra generated ₹250 crore of GDP” confuses reported transactions with value added. “Every Odia is an entrepreneur” is an unsupported generalisation.

Use “participated in exchange,” “commemorates maritime traders,” and “building the next chapter” where those match the evidence. Keep a claim’s scope visible in the main caption rather than relying on an unreadable footnote to repair a misleading headline.
