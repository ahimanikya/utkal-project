# Browse Odisha by subject

Explore Odisha as a place to live, learn, work, create and visit. These nine families organise the encyclopedia. Tourism is one cross-cutting collection; readers can enter through health, education, public finance, industry or history without a visitor itinerary.

- [Places and geography](places.md) — Read Odisha through its districts, cities, coast, wetlands and uplands.
- [History and heritage](history.md) — Explore historical evidence, heritage places and Odisha’s connections with other regions.
- [People and communities](people.md) — Understand who lives in Odisha and how participation differs across groups.
- [Arts and living traditions](arts.md) — Study textiles, crafts, performance and the people who sustain them.
- [Food and farming](food.md) — Connect the food people make with ingredients, farming, fisheries and livestock.
- [Nature and environment](nature.md) — Explore forests, wetlands, biodiversity, water and environmental conditions.
- [Everyday life and human development](everyday.md) — Follow schooling, health, nutrition, work, digital access, basic services and sport as subjects of everyday life.
- [Economy, livelihoods and infrastructure](economy.md) — Examine how Odisha produces, works, finances enterprise and connects with markets.
- [Governance and public life](governance.md) — Read public finance, planning and service delivery through named programmes and institutions.
