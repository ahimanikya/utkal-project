# Mining: national contribution and local development

**Odisha accounted for 43.7% of India’s mineral-production value in IBM’s 2024–25 highlights, excluding fuel, atomic and minor minerals.** The same vintage reports Odisha’s shares of iron-ore and bauxite output as **55%** and **72.7%**. These are different measures; they must not be added or described as a share of all mining including coal.

IBM reports **3.026 million tonnes of chromite**, all from Odisha, with output **down 3.9%** from the preceding year. Preserve that decline alongside the national contribution. These March 2025-vintage figures remain subject to revision. [IBM, printed pp. viii–ix](https://www.ibm.gov.in/writereaddata/files/1748864652683d8e8cdc8f1MSMP_MARCH25FINAL.pdf).

**Mining-area funds need an outcomes story.** CAG records **₹26,602.66 crore in leaseholder contributions** across Odisha’s 30 DMFs during 2015–16 to 2023–24. In its six sampled DMFs, **₹10,092.53 crore** had been spent against **₹20,947.52 crore sanctioned** by March 2024—**48.18%** of sanctions. The sample is Dhenkanal, Jajpur, Keonjhar, Mayurbhanj, Nabarangpur and Sundargarh. These sampled expenditure figures are not statewide utilisation rates or measures of working services. [CAG Report 7 of 2026, executive summary pp. ix–x](https://cag.gov.in/webroot/uploads/download_audit_report/2026/Report-No.-7-of-the-year-2026_PMKKKY-06aba7c29ed78b2.53467972.pdf).

## Evidence boundaries

The IBM value comparison uses nominal reported production values, not real GSVA or government revenue. Resource stocks, production, dispatch, processing, sales and tax receipts need separate series. The CAG report was listed on 28 September 2026 but covers an earlier audit period; it is not a September 2026 balance.
