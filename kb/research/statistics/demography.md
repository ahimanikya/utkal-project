- Historical baseline; not a 2026 population estimate. Social categories are Census classifications and do not count languages or cultural traditions.
- Survey estimate, not Census enumeration; use to understand ageing without converting to headcounts from unrelated population series.
- Not the average number of children already born to all women; not by itself a projection of future population.
