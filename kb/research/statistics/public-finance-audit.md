## Interpretation limits

- Audit flagged revenue-to-capital misclassification and transfers to ledger accounts; do not equate every booked rupee with completed assets.
- Use the adjusted measure when discussing post-audit balances.
- Adjustment incorporates the stated 721 crore misclassification; not a forecast.
- Stock at financial year end; not annual borrowing.
- Use the report GSDP vintage; do not recompute using a different survey revision.
- Lower ratio coexists with higher absolute liabilities.
- Ratio of two annual flows.
- Does not imply interest cost is zero.
