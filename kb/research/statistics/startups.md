# Startups

DPIIT records 3,589 recognised startup entities in Odisha at 31 December 2025. Recognition is an administrative status, not a measure of survival or commercial success. The separate Startup Odisha figures should remain a distinct series.[^enterprise-startups2025]

## What these measures explain

Which enterprises are recognised under each programme? State and central recognition systems have different populations; a woman director or partner does not necessarily mean a woman-founded business.
