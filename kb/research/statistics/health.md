## Interpretation limits

- Coverage of any member does not establish coverage of everyone, claims paid, service quality or protection from all out-of-pocket spending.
- Retrospective five-year birth window; not births in calendar 2024 alone or a direct safety/quality rating.
- NFHS-6 comparison column gives NFHS-5 as 90.7%; original NFHS-5 factsheet reported 90.5%. Preserve edition difference; do not infer meaningful improvement from 0.1 point without uncertainty analysis.
