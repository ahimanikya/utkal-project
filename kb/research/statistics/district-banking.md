# District Banking

## What these measures explain

Where are deposits and advances recorded across Odisha’s districts? Banking location is a useful lens on finance, but it does not measure district wealth or district GDP.

All 30 districts have deposit and utilised-credit balances at June 2026, plus reported June 2025 and June 2026 credit-deposit ratios. This is one domain of district coverage, not complete district profiles. Banking location is not a measure of household wealth.
