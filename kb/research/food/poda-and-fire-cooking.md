## What the connection means

Direct roasting exposes vegetables to heat before peeling/mashing. Leaf parcels enclose food and can combine surface charring with moisture retained inside. Bamboo is a cooking vessel in the meat account. Baking gives rice cakes and cheese sweets another route to a browned surface. These are related for this editorial collection; they are not interchangeable definitions of barbecue.

[Pakhala](pakhala.md) supplies an everyday meal connection through Sasmita’s baigana recipe. [Poda pitha](poda-pitha.md) supplies festival context through the saved Raja account. [Chhena poda](chhena-poda.md) lets the story finish with a sweet. This is an editorial tasting sequence, not a documented traditional set meal or bookable tour.

[Enduri pitha](enduri-pitha.md) is a useful boundary: it is leaf-wrapped and steamed. Leaves alone do not make a dish barbecue. Lopa’s account also describes a modern enclosed daba-poda adaptation; the name alone does not prove charring. The food traditions shared with neighbouring regions must retain their own credits; resemblance does not establish migration or historical influence.
