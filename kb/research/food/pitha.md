# Pitha: a collection, not one dish

Odisha’s pitha repertoire includes pan-cooked, boiled, baked and fried preparations.[^food-tourism]

For the website, make this a browsable collection. Begin with [chakuli](chakuli.md), [poda](poda-pitha.md), [enduri](enduri-pitha.md), [sijha manda](manda-pitha.md), [kakara](kakara-pitha.md) and [arisa](arisa-pitha.md). These are examples, not an inventory of all Odia pitha.

## Editorial proposal

“How many ways can a home say welcome?” is a stronger opening than a large unverified dish count. Each page should show a whole serving, a texture close-up and the maker’s hands. Let the cook identify the occasion, ingredients and variation. A six-part series can introduce one preparation at a time and end with a tasting spread.

## Recipe research template

Capture local name and approved spelling; place; contributor credit and consent; ingredient quantities; preparation method; household variation; occasion; serving context; photography rights; and dietary/allergen information verified from that recipe. Do not infer that every version of a dish has the same ingredients.
