# Bela pana: a summer drink and an Odia New Year connection

Bela pana brings ripe bael fruit into Odisha’s summer-drink repertoire. The ingredient is **bela, *Aegle marmelos***. The saved [Balasore fruit study](../sources/food-koli-balasore2020.md), Table 1 row 1, records its pulp in sherbet and squash; row 42 separately identifies kaitha as *Limonia acidissima*. English “wood apple” labels alone should not merge these fruits.

## How one home version is made

[Sasmita Sahoo’s 2021 account](https://rosheighara.wordpress.com/2021/04/16/4-traditional-healthy-drinks-of-odisha/) places Bela pana among summer drinks. Her version uses one bael fruit, five glasses of water, sugar or mishri, and black pepper.

1. Dissolve the sweetener in water.
2. Open the fruit and scoop out its pulp.
3. Work the pulp into the water and separate the seeds.
4. Strain; finish each serving with pepper. Ice is optional in this account.

**Quantity gaps:** glass size and sweetener amount are unspecified. The ingredient list gives a “small tablespoon” of pepper, while the method says a pinch per serving. Keep that discrepancy visible: this is an attributed method, not a calibrated or kitchen-tested Utkal recipe.

## More than one preparation

[Chef Reetu Uday Kugaji’s account](https://chefreetuudaykugaji.com/blog/2023/03/06/bael-ka-sharbat-bel-ka-sharbatwood-apple-sharbat%EF%BF%BC/) describes an Odisha version with pepper, grated coconut, mashed banana and chhena alongside strained bael, water and sugar or jaggery. This is a credited variation, not a rule for every household. Her separate measured sharbat-concentrate recipe is not relabelled as this Bela pana version.

## Summer and Odia New Year

[Fakir Mohan University’s report](https://fmuniversity.nic.in/getdata?dir=InstituteNews&rid=inewsEvent75_15420231681554279989.pdf) documents Bela pana at its **14 April 2023 Odia New Year / Maha Bishuba Sankranti celebration** in Nuapadhi, Balasore. This establishes a specific cultural example, not participation totals or one obligatory festival recipe.

The Balasore field study reports May–June for Bela fruiting within its January 2018–February 2020 survey of five blocks. That source-specific season is not a statewide retail calendar, and an April festival serving does not establish an April local harvest.
