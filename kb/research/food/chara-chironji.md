# Chara koli and chironji: fruit, seed and kernel

**A fruit name and a traded kernel can tell different parts of the same plant's story.** Odisha's existing Koli register identifies chara koli as *Buchanania lanzan*. ICAR-CIAH uses that botanical name for chironji. This links the names through their reported plant identity; it does not establish that every lot sold as “char seed” is a shelled kernel.

## A seasonal food account

Noor and Satapathy's **2018–2020 Balasore survey** gives **April–May** as the fruiting period. It records kernels eaten raw or roasted and used in sweet preparations. No specific Odisha sweet recipe, proportions or roasting endpoint is supplied. [Table 1, row 15, printed p.451](https://www.botanyjournals.com/assets/archives/2020/vol5issue3/5-3-106-999.pdf).

## Community knowledge and trade

SCSTRTI's study lists **Char Seed / Char Manji, Buchanania lanzan**, among market products for **Juang communities in Hatisila, Nadam and Sarai** (Table 2.16, printed p.49). It also records summer chara fruit/seed use in its Hill Kharia–Mankirdia food table (Table 3.7, printed p.63). The study date remains unresolved; these accounts establish attributed historical uses, not present sales, income or universal practice. [Source](https://repository.tribal.gov.in/bitstream/123456789/74427/1/SCST_2014_research_0338.pdf).

Keep **whole fruit → hard seed/nut → separated kernel** distinct when collecting weights and prices. Shelling yield, breakage, labour and grade would explain value addition, but no Odisha measurements have yet been established.

## Cultivars and processing research

ICAR-CIAH lists **Thar Priya** under chironji. This is a documented cultivar, not evidence that it grows commercially in Odisha. The field-study wild-food classification likewise cannot certify the origin of purchased nuts. [SVRC row 3](https://www.icar-ciah.org/index.php?do=veriti_relese_svrc&mod=veriti).

A **2012 processing paper** is bibliographically located, but the original page and PDF were unavailable. Its indexed abstract is retained as a follow-up lead; its machinery, oil-content and market claims are not adopted as current local facts. [Publisher record](https://nopr.niscpr.res.in/handle/123456789/13445).
