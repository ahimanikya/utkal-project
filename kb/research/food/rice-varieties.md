# Odisha’s rice: varieties, seed keepers and food

Rice connects Odisha’s farms, kitchens, plant science and trade. This collection begins with **21 named entries**, not a count of all varieties grown in the state. The [structured register](../references/data/rice-economy.json) records whether evidence concerns cultivation, conservation or release.

## Heritage kept alive by farmers

MSSRF describes **Tulasa Ghiuria of Nuaguda, Kundura, Koraput**, conserving more than 70 indigenous rice seeds. It names Kalajeera, Dangarbasumti, Machakanta, Tikkichudi, Haldichudi and Muktabali. Its account specifically mentions cultivating the first three; it is undated and cannot establish 2026 acreage. [Institutional profile](https://www.mssrf.org/our-stories/more-than-70-landraces-of-paddy-varieties-have-been-conserved-by-tulasa).

Reuse the existing [Koraput Kalajeera profile](koraput-kalajeera-rice.md) for the saved GI record. Similar names alone do not prove a seed lot’s identity, geographical certification or entitlement to use a GI.

## A heritage variety and its improved selection are different entries

NRRI identifies **Nua Kalajeera, Nua Dhusara and Nua Chinikamini** as pureline selections from Kalajeera, Dhusara and Chinikamini respectively. The parent landrace and selected variety must retain separate identities. [Research Bulletin 39, printed p.4](https://icar-crri.in/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/NRRI-Research-Bulletin-39.pdf).

The same bulletin separates Geetanjali’s 2005 release from its 2006 notification; the HTML list labels 2006 as release. Both statements are retained in the register, with a notification check still open. No single harmonised date is asserted.
