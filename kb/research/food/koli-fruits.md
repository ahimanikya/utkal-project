# Koli: Odisha’s local fruit names and varieties

Koli is a useful local-language doorway into Odisha’s fruit diversity. The names below span several botanical groups; they are not cultivars of a single fruit. Preserve the place and source behind each name.

## One local name can point to different plants

RPRC uses **Khirakoli** for *Carissa spinarum*. The Balasore table labels *Manilkara hexandra* **Khira-koli** and calls *Carissa spinarum* **Anka-koli**. This overlap needs local spelling and specimen checks; the records remain separate.

## Food, markets and conservation

RPRC connects wild fruits with village markets and maintains a conservation collection. Its institutional holdings do not establish how many koli varieties Odisha has. The Balasore study records pickle uses for bara, pharsa and guala; exact preparations require credited recipe evidence.

[Patra and Basak’s 2020 pectin study](https://www.foodandnutritionjournal.org/volume8number2/physicochemical-characterization-of-pectin-extracted-from-six-wild-edible-fruits-in-odisha-india/) supplies another Karanda koli account and a possible processing-research connection. Laboratory findings are not evidence of sales or exports.
