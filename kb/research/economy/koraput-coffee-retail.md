# Koraput coffee: a sealed-pack retail pilot

**Desk-research checkpoint · 2 October2026.** Proposed model: one physical retail point in Koraput district reselling original sealed packs bought from a traceable producer/roaster/distributor. Own roasting, grinding, repacking, branding, drinks service, online selling and export are outside this first model. Those activities change costs and may change permissions.

## Why investigate it?

Coffee Board reports **470MT for Odisha in2025–26 as a final production estimate**, alongside **550MT for2026–27 as a post-blossom estimate**. These establish a state production context; the later number is a forecast, not achieved growth or a supplier's available stock. Neither is a Koraput district total. The page's physical product basis needs confirmation before comparison with cherry/fruit procurement. [Production source](../sources/coffee-board-production2026.md).

The business question is whether a small retail point can sell traceable local coffee at a contribution sufficient to cover rent, labour and stock losses. Visitors and local home-brewers are proposed customer segments. No customer survey, sales ledger, footfall conversion or willingness-to-pay evidence has yet been obtained. Product presence is not an unmet market.

[Order, PDFp1](../sources/enterprise-fssai-order2026.md) and [eligibility, PDFp6; fee header p1](../sources/enterprise-foscos-eligibility2026.md) were checked. These are national published criteria, not approval of a particular applicant or a complete startup budget. The same table treats e-commerce separately; do not apply the offline retailer row automatically to online trading. The effective turnover change supersedes older turnover guidance; a fresh application still needs the selected premises and complete activity assessment.

## Premises decision

Investigate a compliant leased retail unit or an agreed counter within an existing compatible shop. These are model options, not identified vacancies. A land purchase or industrial allotment is not assumed. Record exact site, permitted use, rent/deposit quote, electricity, safe dry storage, access, lease restrictions and relevant local authority before estimating costs. [GO PLUS](../sources/enterprise-goplus-manual.md) remains a broader industrial-site lead, not evidence that this retailer needs industrial land.
