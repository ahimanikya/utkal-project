# Odisha’s GDP story: scale, momentum and meaning

The most useful economic narrative connects the size of the economy to visible sectors and places. Lead with a clear, dated number and then show what it represents.

## Opening evidence

The preserved survey records give **₹9.88 lakh crore nominal GSDP** and **7.9% real GSDP growth** for FY2025–26, both advance estimates. These are two different measures: [economic size](../evidence/e02.md) and [inflation-adjusted annual growth](../evidence/e01.md). They can sit side by side if labelled properly.

## Suggested message

“Odisha’s next chapter is taking shape.” Follow it with the economic-size number, its estimate label, and a sequence covering ports, production, food, services and people. Treat the phrase as campaign language rather than a statistical finding.

## A richer proof sequence

Use [construction’s five-year real growth](construction.md), [fisheries production](comparison-01-fisheries.md) and [Paradip cargo](comparison-05-port-logistics.md) as distinct stories. Their measures must stay separate: economic value added, physical output and port throughput. They cannot be added into a single “growth” total.

The [industry growth collection](index.md) retains complete six-year sector series so the campaign can use both the positive endpoint comparison and a truthful chart. A changing trajectory is more informative than an invented uninterrupted boom.

## Publication controls

Use a common vintage and price basis for any India comparison. The state survey’s national comparator and later national estimates are not automatically comparable: see [survey comparator](../evidence/e04.md) and [later national estimate](../evidence/e05.md). Do not call nominal expansion real growth. Do not turn per-capita income into a claim about every household. Announced investments and jobs remain announcements until completion is established.

The nominal ten-year tripling angle from earlier work needs its historical starting value added as an independently locatable record before it becomes a website headline.
