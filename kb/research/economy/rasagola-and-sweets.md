## What export evidence currently says

The 27 April release discusses processing, logistics and cold-chain opportunities. This establishes export promotion, **not completed sweet shipments or export revenue**.

## The numbers to collect

| Measure | Required scope |
|---|---|
| Milk and chhena input; sweet output | Producer, period, kg/litres, yield and wastage |
| Sales | Named business or representative survey; domestic and export revenue separate |
| Work and income | Paid workers, family work, seasonality, wages and net income |
| Export shipments | Actual shipment date, producer origin, quantity, value, destination and product identity |
| Costs | Milk, sugar, labour, fuel, packaging, transport and spoilage on the same batch basis |
| Cluster scale | Counted enterprises and sampling method; avoid multiplying a few popular shops by an invented statewide total |

All sweet-specific export quantities, values and destination counts remain unknown in this batch. Overseas online availability is a lead, not proof of an Odisha-origin shipment. Customs categories may aggregate many sweets, and shipments via another state may obscure manufacturing origin. Reconcile these before claiming a global rasagola market size.
