## District and value-chain research

Build profiles around mineral, mine, operator, district, processing plant and transport route. Candidate geographies for verification include Keonjhar/Kendujhar and Sundargarh, the Sukinda/Jajpur area, Angul–Talcher and the Ib Valley, and bauxite-linked areas of Koraput, Rayagada and Kalahandi. This is a research selection, not a verified deposit or active-mine map. Preserve historical and administrative aliases.

Begin with iron ore → beneficiation/pellets → iron and steel; bauxite → alumina → aluminium → fabrication; chromite → ferrochrome → stainless steel; coal → power and industrial uses. These are research pathways, not verified supplier relationships between particular companies. Link plant-level evidence before drawing arrows on a public map.

## What readers should see

Short mineral explainers, district portraits, credited worker and scientist profiles, five- or ten-year charts, and plant-stage timelines. A public card should carry one takeaway, its year/unit and a source reveal. A mine profile should state whether it is proposed, auctioned, leased, operating, suspended or closed, with an observation date.

Keep useful positive achievements alongside setbacks and unresolved questions. More ore extracted does not on its own establish higher local incomes or better services. Green-mining claims need measured outcomes.
