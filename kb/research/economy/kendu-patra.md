# Kendu patra: a leaf, skilled work and trade

**Kendu patra means the leaf product in this collection.** OFDC identifies kendu as *Diospyros melanoxylon* and describes the leaves' use as bidi wrappers. Leaf grading, bundling and trade are distinct from any fruit or food entry. [OFDC](https://www.odishafdc.com/kenduleaf.php).

## The story to document

Follow collection, sorting, drying, grading, storage and sale through named places and workers. OFDC's institutional account describes processed and phal leaf forms and the organisation of marketing following nationalisation in 1973. Further research needs worker-attributed accounts, seasonal calendars and dated collection and payment records. Do not infer every household's practice from an agency description.

[The existing market series](../statistics/forest-products.md) owns the sales figures. Reuse it instead of creating a competing kendu total. Gross sale value, working funds, collector payments and net household income have different meanings; the 2019–20 source discrepancy remains held.
