# Homestays: government support and policy history

A hosted home can connect accommodation spending with local food, crafts and work. Odisha’s support programme creates an opportunity to develop those connections; household income and benefits to makers still need measurement.

## What the 2025 scheme provides

The Gazette defines family-hosted accommodation around the owner’s residence. Its financial route supports selected new units in notified rural areas; other units have a separate non-financial route. The notified framework offers up to **50% of eligible fixed capital investment excluding land**, limited to **₹2.5 lakh per lettable room**, four rooms and **₹10 lakh per unit**. Payment is conditional and staged: 50%, 15%, 15% and 20%, with later booking-night thresholds of 15, 30 and 45 per room. These are historical source provisions, not amounts already paid.[^homestay-scheme2025]

Support also includes promotion, training, mentoring and digital visitor management. The budget window ends FY2028–29, while scheme validity runs to 31 March 2030 unless changed. The programme supersedes the 2021 scheme.[^homestay-scheme2025]

## Digital support: what exists versus what is measured

The public Go Homestay address is saved, but its page returned no readable dashboard data in this run.[^homestay-go-portal] OCAC’s app listing describes application tracking, inspections, incentives, visitor records and reporting. These advertised functions are useful source leads; they were not tested in an authenticated account. App downloads are not counts of hosts, bookings or approved units.[^homestay-go-app]

## National programmes are a separate layer

The national parliamentary reply describes a 1,000-homestay tribal-development intervention and national credit support. Those are India-level programmes, not an Odisha allocation. National classification, state registration, marketing-platform listings and financial-benefit selection need separate labels.[^homestay-nidhi2025]

## Connections to document next

Record host consent, a stable property ID, operating status, room count and date before recommending a stay. A regional food tradition does not prove that a host serves it; a nearby craft centre does not prove a commercial relationship. Research actual meals, guide services, maker visits and purchases through attributed local accounts. No booking recommendation follows from scheme eligibility alone.
