# Sugar factories: output, inputs and changing ownership

**The sugar table offers an eleven-year factory history, but its scope and arithmetic need care.** Odisha Agriculture Statistics 2023–24, table 29, distinguishes cane crushed from sugar produced. Its indexed grand total rises from 26.060 to 28.712 thousand tonnes in 2023–24, a provisional **10.2% increase**, while remaining 52.6% below 2013–14. The original page could not be downloaded or viewed, so these values are research-stage, pending facsimile review. [Existing primary source](../sources/rice-oas2024.md).

The [structured capture](../references/data/food-processing-series.json) retains all eleven year labels, six named factory columns, group totals and symbols. Quantity units are thousand tonnes. ## Keep recovery discrepancies visible

For 2023–24, the indexed table reports grand-total recovery of 17.650%, whereas 28.712 ÷309.255 ×100 gives about 9.28%. Several other rows differ too. The register retains the reported and recomputed numbers as a diagnostic, without replacing official values. Recovery rates are excluded from the atlas pending original-page and methodological reconciliation.

The [Aska operator page](../sources/food-aska-status.md) also reports 60,846 quintals of sugar for 2016–17, equivalent to 6.0846 thousand tonnes; the agriculture table gives 6.137. Both are preserved as a conflict. Undated homepage workforce, membership and capacity claims are not treated as current measurements.

## A factory sale changes the company reporting period

Sakthi’s [2022–23 Board report, printed p.16](../sources/food-sakthi-ar2023.md) reports that its Dhenkanal sugar/distillery units were sold as a going concern on 11 November 2022. It records 13,410 tonnes of cane crushed and 1,901 tonnes of sugar produced up to sale, against 272,344 and 25,077 tonnes in the previous fiscal year. The original page was checked visually.

The partial year cannot form a full-year decline calculation. The state table’s continued historical “Sakti” column does not establish current ownership, and a company’s discontinued-operation classification does not mean the factory closed. The prior-year state and company quantities also differ; exact year definitions and successor reporting remain unresolved. No buyer or current owner is inferred here.
