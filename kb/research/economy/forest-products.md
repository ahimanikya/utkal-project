# Forest products: household use, markets and livelihoods

**The central question is how forest knowledge and produce support everyday life and paid work.** Keep foods connected to the wider non-timber forest-products economy without calling every product food.

| Research group | What to document |
| --- | --- |
| Fruits, kernels, flowers and honey | Identity, use, season, preservation, supply origin and food-processing evidence |
| Leaves and leaf products | Sal-leaf collection, plate-making, labour, local buyers and processing; examine other leaf identities separately |
| Oilseeds, gums, resins and lac | Product and grade, actual end uses, processing yields and prices |
| Bamboo, cane, fibres and brooms | Material, maker, end product and commercial channel |
| Kendu leaves | A non-food commodity used as bidi wrappers; separate leaf trade from any fruit entry |

These groups define research coverage. Inclusion does not establish present procurement, sustainable supply, exports or proven medicinal effects. The TDCCOL product page is currently a partially captured discovery lead, not a verified current buying list.

## Follow value through each transaction

A proposed reporting chain is **collection → household use or local sale → aggregation → processing → retail or export**. Verify actual buyers before claiming a product follows that chain. Record procurement separately from sales, and collector receipts separately from turnover and net earnings. Prices require date, place, grade, moisture and unit. Published support prices do not prove procurement at that price.

[The first market checkpoint](../statistics/forest-products.md) saves a kendu series and one honey-processing capacity observation. It is not an estimate of Odisha's entire forest-food market. [Forest foods](../food/forest-foods.md) retains household and culinary context; [history](../history/forest-livelihoods.md) provides dated evidence rather than invented ancient origins.

## Next evidence to obtain

Product-level TDCCOL/Van Dhan procurement and realised sales; collector payments and costs; women-led groups and named community enterprises; seasonal haat prices; storage loss and processing yields; state-origin export quantities. Export promotion, an online listing or an overseas buyer mention alone does not establish measured shipments. For land, collection rights and ecological effects, use current primary decisions and location-specific studies.

## Leaves are a material economy too

[Sal](sal-products.md) and [siali](siali-products.md) now distinguish tree and climber, leaf and seed/fibre, maker processes and local seasonal accounts. Collection, stitching, pressing and selling need separate measures. Current market totals and exports remain open; the historical craft evidence does not fill those gaps.

## Fruit and kernel value chains
