# From mineral revenues to working community services

A mine can generate revenue, a fund can pay a contractor, and a village can still be waiting for a working service. This research follows the steps between those events. It is a draft evidence account, with dated findings and government replies, not a present-day verdict on a district.

## Follow each rupee without skipping a stage

State mining receipts and DMF contributions are separate financial routes. Within the DMF route, follow sanctioned projects, released funds, expenditure, completed assets, operating services and community outcomes as distinct stages. OMC dividends, mine-level payment returns and DMF interest also need separate labels.

The Directorate’s 2023–24 mining collection series reports 46,396.27. The 0.73 crore difference is compatible with rounding in magnitude but the accounting-head bridge has not been established; retain both vintages and definitions. Dividends are excluded. The legacy [F05](../evidence/f05.md) figure of 53,000 crore is a 2026–27 **budget estimate**. It remains intact and cannot establish actual collections or a realised increase over 2024–25.

Completion says nothing by itself about staffing, utilities, sustained use or residents’ welfare. These six districts were audit-selected; neither their spending ratio nor these selected failures estimates all Odisha DMFs.

## Four cases: completion, use and the reply

Each case below can include more than one facility. Combined costs must not be repeated as per-facility costs. All four are audit-selected problem cases; they are not a balanced evaluation of the programme.

### Dedicated NICU/PICU at District Headquarters Hospital, Keonjhar

Equipment procured January–February 2022 and installed March 2022 according to CDM&PHO records cited by Audit. Equipment approved and paid during December 2021–June 2022: ₹2.18 crore.

Joint physical verification on 29 April 2025 found units not operational and equipment unused; staff/doctors had not been deployed.

**Government reply:** January 2026: government said the assets strengthened crisis healthcare infrastructure. **Audit response:** CAG said the reply did not address non-use for the purchased purpose, continuing through January 2026.

**Next evidence:** Dated commissioning and duty-roster records, de-identified admission/service aggregates and independent patient/community accounts. Current operation in October 2026 is unverified. Locator: §6.6.3, printed p. 60 / PDF p. 98.

### Boys and girls hostels, Deogan village, Sukinda block

Completion certificate dated 16 July 2022 from R&B Division, Panikoili. Combined construction cost; do not assign the full amount to each hostel: ₹4.34 crore.

Not handed over to Education Department; joint verification 10 December 2024 found non-functional buildings, with water and electricity pending.

**Government reply:** January 2026: handover to BDO Sukinda would occur once critical utilities were in place. **Audit response:** CAG maintained the hostels had remained non-functional since completion.

**Next evidence:** Utility connections, handover/occupation certificates, staffing and anonymised occupancy by term. Current operation in October 2026 is unverified. Locator: §6.6.6, printed p. 62 / PDF p. 100.

### Digital medical records in DHH Sundargarh, SDH Bonai and Government Hospital Rourkela

Installed September 2019 at Bonai and November 2019 at the other two hospitals. Combined payment for system/equipment at three hospitals; sanction 1.52 crore and release 1.33 crore are separate stages: ₹1.32 crore.

Initially operated; audit records end dates 25 February 2020 at Sundargarh/Rourkela and 28 December 2020 at Bonai. Verification 4 March 2025 at Sundargarh found non-functioning system and 24 of 40 tablets stored unused.

**Government reply:** January 2026: acknowledged non-functioning since March 2020 and unsuccessful vendor engagement on compatibility with OeHMIS. **Audit response:** CAG attributed the failure to unresolved compatibility and ineffective use of warranty support; ₹1.32 crore termed infructuous. The general government reply date does not erase the hospital-specific dates.

**Next evidence:** System migration/asset redeployment records and dated evidence of records successfully used in care; do not infer all healthcare at these hospitals stopped. Current operation in October 2026 is unverified. Locator: §6.6.4, printed pp. 60–61 / PDF pp. 98–99.

### Wholesale market complex and Mission Shakti Café, Vyasanagar/Jajpur Road

Sanctioned in 2021–22/2022–23; audit describes completion and handovers in February/November 2023. Combined completed cost of two facilities; not separate per-facility amounts: ₹6.39 crore.

Joint verification November 2024 found intended beneficiary allotments absent and facilities not in use.

**Government reply:** January 2026: construction completed and facility handed to KNDA on 25 November 2023 for operation/management. **Audit response:** CAG retained its finding of defunct facilities; the handover reply does not establish operation.

**Next evidence:** Allotment and opening records, dated activity, tenant/SHG revenue and beneficiary testimony, with costs split by facility. Current operation in October 2026 is unverified. Locator: §6.6.9, printed p. 66 / PDF p. 104.

## What would establish benefit?

A completed hostel needs functioning water/electricity, staff and evidence of occupation. An ICU needs staff and evidence of care delivered. A market needs occupied stalls and trading activity. A digital system needs evidence of use in care. These are proposed verification fields, not findings that the evidence already supplies.

For each case retain: stable identity and geography; funding source; sanction/release/expenditure dates; completion and handover records; operation dates; maintenance/staffing; de-identified service counts; intended and actual beneficiary scope; and attributed community accounts. An outcome claim needs an appropriate baseline/comparison and cannot be inferred solely from use. Never collect identifiable patient or student records for this research.

Chapter VII records weak public disclosure and absent asset registers in the audited sample. January 2026 replies describe training and progressive asset-register compilation; these are reported responses, not independently verified remediation. The present reading covers the selected original sections and responses listed in the source capture, not every audit chapter.
