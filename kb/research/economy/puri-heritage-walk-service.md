# Puri: a pre-booked outdoor heritage-walk service

**Research checkpoint · 3 October 2026.** OPP-PUR-001; RES-066 remains in progress. No priced, approved or bookable Utkal service is offered.

## Customer problem and business hypothesis

Investigate whether visitors already staying in Puri would pay for a pre-booked, guided explanation of its public streets, architecture and cultural history. A local guide-led service could sell directly or through an agreed hotel/tour-operator referral. These are proposed customer/channel choices; no agreement or demand test exists.

The initial scope is an outdoor walk. Temple entry, ritual services, food preparation/sales, transport and craft workshops are separate activities requiring their own evidence. No privileged access is promised. An exact route, duration, meeting point, group size and accessible alternative remain to be checked with local participants.

## What the evidence establishes

The [original OTDC RFQ of 14 September 2022](../sources/enterprise-otdc-walk-rfq2022.md) demonstrates institutional interest in purchasing heritage-walk services. It required route scripts, certified professional guides and relevant permissions. It specified at least two hours and up to 20 people per guide for that contract; these are historical specifications, not general operating rules for every Puri walk.

Crucially, Annexure 1 lists **Bhubaneswar**, with 80 expected and 30 minimum guaranteed walks annually. Puri appears as a possible additional location. This is not evidence of guaranteed Puri revenue, an awarded contract, delivered tours or a live tender. Its quoted-price form is blank. An unavailable 2023 document is kept separate; the 2022 edition does not establish its terms.

A [commercial service page](../sources/enterprise-puri-walk-offer.md) advertises Puri guided walks. It provides evidence of a marketed category, not sales or a verified operator recommendation. No visible walk tariff was found. Its top panel names Bhubaneswar alongside October–March and 2–4 hours, so those figures must not be silently reused for Puri.

## Seasonality and demand remain open

No Puri walk booking series, monthly paid attendance, conversion rate or customer interviews have been obtained. Statewide tourist visits and festival crowds cannot substitute for paying customers. Season labels on a different city's offer cannot supply an off-season forecast.

Needed evidence: date-specific enquiries and bookings from a consenting provider, cancellations, chosen-language demand, price paid, party size and seasonal operating constraints. Separate free community walks from commercial purchases. Retain months with low or no activity rather than extrapolating a peak period.

Current guide/agency recognition, municipal trade classification, exact route jurisdiction, group assembly, photography, insurance and tax/employment applicability still need authoritative confirmation.
