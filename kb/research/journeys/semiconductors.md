# Odisha’s semiconductor journey

## The reader’s takeaway

**Two Odisha projects approved under the India Semiconductor Mission in August 2025.**

SiCSem covers silicon-carbide compound semiconductors; 3D Glass Solutions covers advanced packaging and embedded glass substrates. Approval identifies a developing pipeline, not two factories already producing commercially.[^legacy-ism]

## Milestones

- **12 August 2025 — approval:** the Union Cabinet approved SiCSem and 3D Glass Solutions projects in Odisha. SiCSem’s planned annual capacity was 60,000 wafers.[^legacy-ism]
- **19 April 2026 — groundbreaking:** the HIPS/3D Glass project at Info Valley reached its foundation-stone event. Reported project investment: **₹1,943.53 crore**. Commercial production was targeted for August 2028, with full-volume production targeted for August 2030.[^legacy-glass]
- **15 September 2026 — separate SEZ approval:** SiCSem’s proposal recorded **₹3,406.25 crore** investment and approximately **1,270 expected jobs**. These remain a proposal value and employment projection.[^legacy-sicsez]
- **18 September 2026 — wider pipeline:** MeitY reported approximately **₹23,600 crore of investment proposals**, 6,100 potential employment opportunities and an announced 870-acre Odisha Silicon Valley at Naraj. These are ecosystem proposals and an announced development.[^journey-semicon-2026]

## Meaning and presentation

Use a milestone ribbon with explicit labels: **approved → groundbreaking → construction evidence → commissioned → commercial production**. Put future targets on a visibly separate timeline. A later approval can concern an existing project rather than a new factory.

Do not add the broad September proposal total to named project estimates: overlap has not been reconciled. Do not add SiCSem’s separate approval figures as if each were additional investment. Keep planned jobs, realised headcount, installed capacity and actual output in separate fields.

The earlier [SiCSem evidence records](../evidence/s02.md) use another approval-context investment figure; its scope difference from the SEZ proposal remains unresolved. The [saved RIR update](../evidence/s11.md) remains a secondary account pending the underlying company filing. The new primary sources checked here do not establish commercial output at those facilities.

## What to track next

Project-specific construction and commissioning evidence; production-start disclosures; actual spending; employment; local supplier links; skills programmes and outcomes. Preserve the distinction between manufacturing, packaging, design and training.
