## Milestones

- **28 January 2016:** Bhubaneswar led the first group of 20 Smart City Challenge winners. The distinction concerns selection proposals.[^journey-smart-selection]
- **20 September 2016:** Rourkela was selected, according to Odisha Tourism.[^journey-rourkela-selection]
- **20 January 2025 snapshot:** Bhubaneswar reported 31 of 32 projects completed; Rourkela 73 of 75. Together, 104 of 107.[^journey-smart-2025]
- **15 January 2026 snapshot:** the later parliamentary answer again reported 104 of 107 completed.[^journey-smart-2026]

- **1 March 2026 snapshot:** Bhubaneswar remained at 31 of 32, Rourkela at 73 of 75; three projects remained at implementation stage. The March 16 answer supplies this later snapshot.[^weekly-smart-mar2026]

## Meaning and presentation

Use a short selection-to-delivery timeline and two city cards. Label the latest city-level counts **1 March 2026** and retain the earlier snapshots. The saved snapshots show no increase in the combined completed-project count from January 2025 through March 2026. Avoid an upward chart implying otherwise.

The editorial opportunity is to connect named completed projects to streets, public spaces, services and resident voices. Until outcome evidence is collected, project completion cannot support a claim that Odisha is India’s cleanest, safest or most liveable state. The 2016 selection result is not a present-day national ranking.

## What to track next

Current status of the remaining projects; usage and maintenance of completed assets; public-transport reach and reliability; pedestrian access; water and sanitation outcomes; planning participation. Keep Smart Cities Mission, AMRUT, sanitation programmes and town-planning schemes as separately labelled programmes.
