# Odisha garments, cloth and markets

A garment is what someone wears; fibre, weaving technique, named tradition and production place describe different aspects of it. Keep these separate so that a Sambalpuri saree, a shirt made from Bandha fabric and factory-produced apparel are not counted as the same product.
