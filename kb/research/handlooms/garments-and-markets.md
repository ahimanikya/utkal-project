# Odisha garments, cloth and markets

A garment is what someone wears; fibre, weaving technique, named tradition and production place describe different aspects of it. Keep these separate so that a Sambalpuri saree, a shirt made from Bandha fabric and factory-produced apparel are not counted as the same product.

Boyanika’s checked navigation offers research leads for **sarees, three-piece dress materials, kurtis, waistcoats, kurtas, shirts, jackets, dupattas/scarves, shawls and stoles**. Running fabric, home textiles and accessories are additional categories. Navigation establishes advertised categories, not stock, garment origin or export shipments.
