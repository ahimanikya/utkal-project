### Useful evidence gaps for a producer-led support pack

**Product measurements need units.** The Prakruti illustration is labelled length 15, width 5 (PDF 22/printed 20), without a unit beside those values. We do not choose inches or centimetres. Other manuscript dimensions elsewhere have units but concern different examples; they cannot supply the missing unit or become mandatory dimensions for every product.

**A local material name needs identification.** PDF 72/printed 70 calls a leaf “KESARADA” and claims it is confined to the Puri–Khordha area. The vernacular label and photograph do not establish a scientific identity, actual distribution or geographic exclusivity. That claim remains a research lead.
