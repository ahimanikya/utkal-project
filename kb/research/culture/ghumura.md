# Ghumura

**Ghumura is a strong entrance into Kalahandi’s performance culture.** The district administration describes its performance at Nuakhai, Dushara and other festivals. Its table gives **18–20 performers per troupe** as a descriptive format, not a district employment count or a rule for every troupe. [District table, row01](https://kalahandi.odisha.gov.in/en/tourism/culture-Heritage).

The Ministry of Culture’s **1 March 2024** panel links **Dhyanananda Panda / PRATIVA (A Tribal Folk Akademy), Bhawanipatna**, to a Ghumura clip. The saved register therefore offers a named research route. We have not verified playback or corrected ambiguous characters in the extracted video identifier. [Register, p.255](https://www.indiaculture.gov.in/sites/default/files/pdf/Folk_Dance_as_on_01.03.2024-223-255.pdf).
