# Ranapa

**Ranapa brings the balance and movement of stilts into Odisha’s performance traditions.** Odisha Tourism’s account, spelt Ranappa there, associates it with Ganjam and episodes from Krishna’s childhood. The description is cultural context; its general visitor suggestions do not verify a performance available today. [Tourism account](https://apps.odishatourism.gov.in/folk-dance).

There is also a concrete national-stage record: Sangeet Natak Akademi’s **2016–17 annual report**, p.32, lists **D. Balaram Reddy and Group, Ganjam**, for Ranapa on **15 December 2016** at Desaj, Gandhi Stadium, Itarsi, Madhya Pradesh. This is the organizer’s retrospective programme record, not an independently observed performance or measured audience.
