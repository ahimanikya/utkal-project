# Tantric traditions: sites, texts and interpretations

**Begin with identifiable places and attributed scholarship.** Odisha Tourism describes Hirapur’s Chausathi Yogini temple as circular and open to the sky, dates it to the ninth century, and attributes it to Queen Hiradevi. These are the tourism publisher’s historical attributions, pending archaeological and epigraphic review. [Official description](https://odishatourism.gov.in/content/tourism/en/discover/attractions/temples-monuments/chausathi-yogini.html).

Its reference to Ranipur-Jharial opens a second site for research. We do not adopt its nationwide surviving-temple total, current event calendar or speculative explanations of ritual practice.

Research should distinguish Yogini/Shakta and Shaiva traditions from Buddhist Vajrayana; a shared word such as tantra does not make them one religion. Follow named deities, dated sculptures, inscriptions, texts and scholarly interpretations. Explore documented connections with Jagannath traditions without assuming a single origin. Avoid sensational descriptions or invented secret practices.
