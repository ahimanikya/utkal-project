# Odisha’s universities: institutions and evidence

**Compare institutions by their role, legal category and dated evidence.** The [Higher Education Department directory](https://dhe.odisha.gov.in/en/universities/list-of-univerisities-in-odisha) supplies the following starting selection, not a best-university league table or a complete statewide census.

| Directory category | Selected institutions to profile |
| --- | --- |
| State public | Utkal, Ravenshaw, Sambalpur, Berhampur, OUAT, Utkal University of Culture, Odisha State Open University, National Law University Odisha |
| Central | Central University of Odisha |
| State private | Centurion, Sri Sri, Birla Global, ASBM, GIET, Silicon |
| Deemed to be university | KIIT, Siksha ‘O’ Anusandhan, KISS |

Deemed status is a legal category; it does not itself establish government ownership. Directory inclusion does not verify current programme approval, admissions, accreditation or every institutional name change. IIT, NIT, NISER, IISER and AIIMS coverage needs a separate institution-type check.

**One dated finding:** NIRF’s **2025 University category** places **Siksha ‘O’ Anusandhan 15th** (63.14) and **KIIT 17th** (62.87). These are India ranks in that category, not ranks among all institutions or subject-specific outcomes. [Original ranking table](https://www.nirfindia.org/Rankings/2025/UniversityRanking.html).

The attempted 2026 table could not be retrieved. These numbers are explicitly 2025; no latest-ranking claim is made.
