# Prathamastami

Enduri’s leaf wrapping connects a food technique with an occasion centred on the eldest child.

**Early research notes: history and local narrative remain incomplete**. Traditional month: Margasira. No annual visit date is confirmed.

## Family and food

Mahanti describes honouring the eldest child, including new clothes and an offering of enduri. Odisha Tourism independently associates enduri with Prathamastami and describes turmeric-leaf wrapping with a coconut-jaggery filling. These support a cultural food connection, not a claim that enduri is unavailable at other times.

Sources: [Fading Festivals of Odisha: A peep into the religious practices in Odisha](https://www.esamskriti.com/essays/pdf/17%20FINAL%20Fading%20Festivals%20of%20Odisha%20%281%29.pdf), [The Taste of Odisha](https://odishatourism.gov.in/content/tourism/en/the-taste-of-odisha.html). ## What the leaf contributes

The existing enduri page is the preparation reference. The observance page asks a different question: how does making, offering and sharing that food express a family relationship? We should record the leaf, batter, filling, steamer and cook alongside the person being honoured, rather than allow the recipe to replace the occasion.
