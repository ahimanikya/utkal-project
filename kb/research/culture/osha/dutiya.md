# Dutiya Osha

The path from offered produce to a shared ghanta is the beginning of this food story.

**Early research notes: history and local narrative remain incomplete**. Traditional month: Ashwina. No annual visit date is confirmed.

## A documented sequence

Dash’s 2006 essay records fruits and vegetables offered during Dutiya Osha and cooked into ghanta the following day. He also mentions sand figures of a fox and a bird. The source groups several names together; we have not treated that as proof that every regional Jitiya or Puajiuntia tradition is identical.

Sources: [The Perishing Tradition of Osha, Brata and Mela in Orissa: An Overview](https://magazines.odisha.gov.in/Orissareview/April2006/engpdf/The_perishing_tradition_of_osha.pdf). Printed pp.15,17.

## Food after the offering

The important research unit is the sequence: gathering, offering, cooking and sharing. A recipe alone would miss the change in the produce’s ritual role. The next account should identify a particular household’s ghanta, its ingredients and recipients; it should also record omissions and substitutions. This is an editorial approach, not a measured claim about waste reduction or nutrition.
