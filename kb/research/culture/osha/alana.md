# Alana Osha

A food restriction can be as culturally meaningful as a festive dish.

**Early research notes: history and local narrative remain incomplete**. Traditional month: Ashwina. No annual visit date is confirmed.

## Agreement and disagreement

Dash describes Alana as an Ashwina observance without salt and associates it with Lakshmi. Mahanti also describes unsalted offerings but names Surya. This is an unresolved source difference; neither a single deity nor a uniform household practice is asserted.
