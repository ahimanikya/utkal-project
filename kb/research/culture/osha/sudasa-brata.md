# Sudasa Brata

A knotted thread and an offering of manda connect a recurring vow with Lakshmi.

**Narrative research checkpoint**. Traditional month: recurring calendar condition, not one fixed month. No annual visit date is confirmed.

## The vow and its objects

The Mohantys describe a thread with ten knots, each associated with a name of Lakshmi. Mahanti gives the calendrical condition as a Thursday coinciding with the bright fortnight’s tenth lunar day; it can recur within a year. Dash’s Kartika-only description is therefore retained as a conflict, not used as the general rule.

Sources: [Laxmi: The Goddess of Prosperity and Salvation](https://magazines.odisha.gov.in/Orissareview/2021/June-July/engpdf/page-21-25.pdf), [Fading Festivals of Odisha: A peep into the religious practices in Odisha](https://www.esamskriti.com/essays/pdf/17%20FINAL%20Fading%20Festivals%20of%20Odisha%20%281%29.pdf), [The Perishing Tradition of Osha, Brata and Mela in Orissa: An Overview](https://magazines.odisha.gov.in/Orissareview/April2006/engpdf/The_perishing_tradition_of_osha.pdf). Mohanty p.25; Mahanti p.43; Dash p.15.

## Manda in the offering

Dash names manda for Sudasa. The existing food record describes a steamed version, but that does not establish the recipe of every ritual offering. The useful questions concern dough, filling, cooking method, number and distribution. A dish name can remain stable while its preparation varies.

Sources: [The Perishing Tradition of Osha, Brata and Mela in Orissa: An Overview](https://magazines.odisha.gov.in/Orissareview/April2006/engpdf/The_perishing_tradition_of_osha.pdf). Printed pp.17–18.

## What history can and cannot say

The sources connect the brata to Lakshmi devotion; they do not establish an earliest Odisha performance in this study. Mahanti’s claim of revival by Balarama Das needs a dated textual witness before becoming our chronology. The knot’s significance should be narrated from an identified katha, rather than completed from an unrelated internet version.

Sources: [Fading Festivals of Odisha: A peep into the religious practices in Odisha](https://www.esamskriti.com/essays/pdf/17%20FINAL%20Fading%20Festivals%20of%20Odisha%20%281%29.pdf). Printed p.43.

## Reading its significance

A useful editorial lens is the relationship between an enduring object and repeated action: a thread can remind its wearer of a vow between observances. This is a question to explore with practitioners, not an assertion that everyone gives the thread the same meaning.
