# Bada Osha

A Shiva offering called gaja needs its own identity, separate from sweets sold under similar names.

## Food with a ritual form

The Jagatsinghpur district gazetteer describes a steamed gaja offering shaped as a linga and snake, followed by the breaking of a fast. This is a district account, not a verified menu at every Shiva temple. The preparation must not be silently equated with the fried chhena gaja in an existing food catalogue.

Sources: [Odisha District Gazetteers: Jagatsinghpur](https://jagatsinghpur.odisha.gov.in/sites/default/files/2023-05/2018051050.pdf). Printed p.52, Bada Osha.
