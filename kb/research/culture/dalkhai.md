# Dalkhai

**Dalkhai connects dance, song and festive community life in western Odisha.** Sambalpur district’s cultural account names young women from Binjhal, Soura and Mirdha communities, dancing in a line or semicircle during Dussehra, Bhaijuntia and other occasions. This is the district’s account, not an exclusive list of communities or a claim that every performance follows one format. [District description](https://sambalpur.odisha.gov.in/en/tourism/culture-Heritage).

A Ministry of Culture panel dated **1 March 2024**, entry592, names **Dhyanananda Panda / PRATIVA (A Tribal Folk Akademy), Bhawanipatna**, and links a Dalkhai clip. This connects the tradition to a credited practitioner/group without assigning them authorship of the whole tradition. The duplicate clip link in the register is one route, not two works. [Register, p.255](https://www.indiaculture.gov.in/sites/default/files/pdf/Folk_Dance_as_on_01.03.2024-223-255.pdf).
