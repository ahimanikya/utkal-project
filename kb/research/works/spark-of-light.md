# Spark of Light: Short Stories by Women Writers of Odisha

**A ready reading route into Odisha’s literature:** this English anthology was published by Athabasca University Press in December 2016, edited by Valerie Henitiuk and Supriya Kar. Its contents list **26 named women authors**, spanning early and later Odia writing. This is the anthology’s coverage, not a statewide count or a popularity ranking.

Paperback ISBN 9781771991674; PDF ISBN 9781771991681; EPUB ISBN 9781771991698. DOI: 10.15215/aupress/9781771991674.01. Editors, story authors and translators have separate roles. Only five selected stories and their author links are indexed individually here so far.
