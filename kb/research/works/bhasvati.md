# Bhāsvatī

A practical astronomical handbook attributed to Śatānanda, dated to 1099 CE in the modern study. Panda describes eight chapters and 128 verses, including calendar, planetary-position and eclipse calculations and a centesimal computational method. These counts describe a text, not a statewide scientific-output measure.

The English-language historical study is available at [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.03661); it is not a complete English translation of the handbook. Manuscript witnesses, edition identity and independent specialist review remain open. Do not claim invention of the decimal system, exact modern accuracy or measured international impact.
