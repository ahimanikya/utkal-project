# The Mendicant

**Read in English:** The Mendicant, by Reba Ray, translated by K. K. Mohapatra, in *Spark of Light* (AU Press, 2016). Credit appears on printed page 3, PDF page 27 (one-based). The volume’s paperback ISBN is **9781771991674** and PDF ISBN **9781771991681**; these identify the anthology, not a separately issued story.

Original language: **Odia**. The anthology introduction and contributor note date Sanyasi to 1899 in Utkal Sahitya. This is distinct from the 2016 English anthology edition.
