## Interpretation for Utkal

Our proposed contribution is to document useful experiences, identify practical barriers and help local enterprises participate. This is an inference from the sources and the Founder's existing direction. Utkal has not been appointed to the reported mapping exercise, promised access to incentives or endorsed by the government.

The [Global Connections model](../../specs/global-connections.md) already distinguishes research from institutional partnership. The [investment tracker methodology](../journeys/investment.md) distinguishes commitments, approvals and outcomes; use the same discipline for tourism support.

## Questions to resolve before public guidance

1. What is the official MICE application URL, linked from an authoritative government page? Can a visitor reach it, and are applications currently accepted? Read-only inspection only; do not submit an application.
2. Which notification and operational guidelines govern event incentives versus capital subsidies? Record effective dates, clauses, applicant types, thresholds, exclusions and any amendment hierarchy.
3. What deadlines, documents and approval-before-event conditions apply? What are the sanctioned, claimed and reimbursed stages?
4. What official help channel can clarify unresolved questions? Record the public channel, but obtain authorization before contacting it.
5. Is there a public brief, implementing owner or participation process for the proposed mapping exercise? Absence of a published brief remains unknown, not proof the exercise does not exist.

Capture each answer with source ID, URL, page/section locator, edition/effective date, checked-at timestamp, claim and uncertainty. Preserve conflicting versions and obtain clarification before offering application guidance. Do not publish unverified subsidy amounts or infer entitlement from this clipping.
