## Official rules checked on 5 October 2026

**OTX-S05 — event incentives.** [MICE and destination-wedding guidelines](https://odishatourism.gov.in/documents/d/guest/grant-of-incentives-to-mice-industry-and-destination-weddings), notification 8434/TSM, 3 June 2026; cites amendment resolution 2154 dated 19 January 2026. All 11 scanned pages inspected. The following is a scoped summary, not an eligibility decision:

- Pages 3–4, §§5–8: MICE covers 1 April–30 September; ₹1,000 per eligible room/day; minimum 100 rooms/day in qualifying accommodation. At least 50% of occupants must be from outside Odisha; support is restricted to their rooms. Weddings have a 50-room/day threshold and additional family-residency conditions. Accommodation recognition/listing conditions apply.
- Pages 4–5, §§9–11: ordinarily apply at least one month before the event. Acknowledgment is not sanction. Submit the post-event claim within 90 days, subject to permitted exceptions; evidence includes occupancy, invoices and payment proof. Verification, sanction and release are separate stages.
- Pages 7–11: application, acknowledgment and claim annexures. The reviewed PDF does not identify an online application URL.

**OTX-S06 — capital investment.** [Capital Investment Subsidy guidelines](https://odishatourism.gov.in/documents/d/guest/disbursement-of-capital-investment-subsidy), notification 8371/TSM, 3 June 2026. Reviewed pages 1 and 3–5 of 30 only: this is support for eligible investment in tourism units, subject to policy definitions, inspection and sanction. Page 4 §8 ordinarily requires the claim within 180 days of commercial operation; operations before 19 January 2026 do not qualify under the 2026 amendment. Page 5 §10 describes application and scrutiny. This is not a complete review of rates, annexures, exclusions or any particular enterprise’s eligibility.

**Implication for our pilot (our assessment):** a small programme of research visits does not establish eligibility under either scheme. Budget it independently. A future conference organiser may have a separate event-incentive case; a qualifying property investor may have a capital case. Do not combine them into a promised subsidy for Utkal, a maker visit or an itinerary.
