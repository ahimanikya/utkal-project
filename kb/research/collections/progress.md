# Odisha progress: what the evidence shows

A promising project can have several different kinds of evidence. Read the status next to the number. These cards retain the source’s observation date; they are not live dashboards or a claim that every project has passed through every stage.

**Planned** describes an intention or target. **Approved** records an administrative decision. **Operating** needs direct dated evidence of activity. **Measured results** need a defined outcome and period. Installed capacity and reported project completion are additional evidence categories: neither automatically establishes operation or impact.

There is no generic progress percentage or statewide investment total here. “Not established in the saved evidence” means unknown, not zero. Existing observation dates are retained; this collection does not recheck every original release.
