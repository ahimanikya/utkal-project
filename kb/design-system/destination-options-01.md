# Three destination-page directions

**Status: A · Visual magazine selected by the Founder; applied locally to Chilika for review.** Ahimanikya Satapathy was not convinced by the page design even after the audit repairs and asked to mock up alternatives before applying a design. This supersedes treating the repaired composition as an accepted design direction.

[Open the local comparison](http://127.0.0.1:4338/). Source: `projects/design-explorations/chilika-01/`, outside the website build. All three use the existing palette, logo, local fonts, credited photographs and Chilika research. Content is intentionally reduced to compare composition rather than reproduce every encyclopedia field.

| Direction | Reading experience | Trade-off to judge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A · Visual magazine | Expressive title, image beside the opening, a paired literary chapter, then places, food and planning | Balanced story and utility; less immediate immersion than B |
| B · Photo story | Broad photographic opening, a centred story and larger images between chapters | Strong mood; takes more scrolling to reach practical choices |
| C · Travel field guide | Quick orientation and shore choices lead; food and culture deepen the visit | Faster research; more structured and less like a continuous essay |

These are whole-page studies: opening, literature, shore choices, food, local legend, seasonal planning, sources and a single footer. Desktop/phone preview controls and separate full-page links support comparison. The comparison viewer itself does not store a choice; the Founder’s later selection is recorded below. Existing guide/planner links open the current local site separately; no new booking or save behaviour is simulated.

Choose an overall composition first. Refine that complete page, then check a food and person page before moving shared patterns into the design system. Avoid combining every attractive section into another long stack.

## Verification and limits

- All three openings inspected in the in-app browser; interior story, shore cards and food/planning compositions inspected.
- Comparison controls verified with keyboard activation; phone frame checked visually.
- Nine browser layout checks: A, B and C at measured 360, 768 and 1440 CSS pixels. No horizontal overflow or failed completed images in those checks.
- Local image/frame links and section anchors resolve across the four review pages.
- These checks establish usable mockups, not human approval, final accessibility conformance or real-device sign-off.
- Existing website source, shared design-system package, research claims and deployment are unchanged in this exploration.
