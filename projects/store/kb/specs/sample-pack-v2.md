# Sample pack v2 — shared artwork and four product sheets

Founder said “Yes, let's do it” to artwork consistency and four product sheets, then directed “The cup should carry some story”. These authorize preparation, not manufacturing, supplier contact or expenditure.

The current art files are versioned under `public/artwork/sea-and-stone-v2/`; the four-page PDF and web design review pair them with schematic placement proofs. All use the same path geometry from the existing book-and-boat vector master and the same outlined Noto wordmarks. English and Odia remain separate. The generated material photographs are concept impressions, not exact print proofs; older v1 files remain intact.

[Structured specifications](sample-specifications-v2.json) are the source of product targets, placements, supplier questions and the cup story. Suggested fabric weights, capacities, sizes and print processes are AI proposals for quoting. Black T-shirt remains the explicit fixed colour; other finishes are proposals. Actual vendor templates and samples are still required. No supplier has been contacted.

The cup is now **The Story Cup**, with “A Book. A Boat. A Journey.” on its reverse and a short Boita Bandana story. The [Ministry of Culture / PIB release, 6 February 2025](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2100365), read in full on 28 September 2026, supports the floating-boat ritual and maritime commemoration. The book-as-sail metaphor is our original interpretation. The story deliberately avoids unsupported claims about a particular voyage, date of origin or depiction of a historic vessel. Final editorial acceptance remains with the Founder.

The PDF has one page per product: consistent artwork, proposed canvas, colour references, placement, proposed blank/material, print process to quote, supplier evidence and a sample request. RGB colours are design references only. Typography is converted to paths, including the cup story. It is not an approved dieline, colour separation or purchase order. No QR code or domain is printed while the public address is undecided.

Next: Founder selects blank targets and placements, vendor supplies printable templates and physical swatches, exact artwork is mapped to those templates, then a dated sample proof receives explicit acceptance. The bottle v2 uses a front-only wave band to avoid inventing a wrap circumference.

## Superseded by graphical story direction

The Founder rejected the prose-on-cup approach: “That English does not make sense - We need use a graphical story”. [Sample pack v3](sample-pack-v3.md) is current. The rejected rendered/print candidate is retained privately in the KB history evidence, not served by the website.
