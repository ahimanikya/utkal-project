# Sample pack v3 — graphical Story Cup

**Current review candidate; not a manufacturing approval.**

Founder correction: “That English does not make sense - We need use a graphical story”. The earlier prose-on-cup interpretation was rejected. The current cup carries an illustrated panorama and a prominent UTKAL wordmark only: small boats launched at dawn, a sea voyage, and a welcoming shore. Source notes and interpretive context stay in the KB and product page, not on the cup.

The [Ministry of Culture release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2100365) supports maritime commemoration and the floating-boat ritual. It does not authenticate the specific people, vessel, clothing, coastline, cargo or sequence in the generated art. The wrap is contemporary illustrative storytelling, not documentary reconstruction or a copied traditional artwork.

Three other products retain exact shared emblem geometry and outlined English/Odia lettering. The cup intentionally has a distinct pictorial design. Its flat wrap includes the original generated 1923 × 818 PNG unchanged, with a prominent 72 mm wide outlined Utkal wordmark. It provides about 244 ppi at the proposed 200 × 85 mm canvas. It is raster art inside an SVG container, not a fully vector illustration or a guaranteed production resolution. Vendor must assess the actual print process, handle gap, bleed, colour and physical proof. The two-view mug rendering is an impression and is not an exact simulated wrap mapping.

[Structured specifications](sample-specifications-v3.json) remain proposals: materials, capacities, size targets, placements, quote fields and samples. Only the black T-shirt base is confirmed. The four-page printable pack and current downloads use v3. V1 is preserved; rejected v2 is retained in internal history evidence. No vendor was contacted and no order, spend, launch or final approval is claimed.

AI assistance: built-in image generation for the new wrap and cup rendering; exact prompts in [the generation record](../records/graphical-cup-prompts-v3.json). Creative direction and final authority: Ahimanikya Satapathy. Font creators retain credit.

## Brand prominence and black bottle refinement

The Founder clarified: “Utkal also has to be prominent - not all image”, then requested: “Refine the water bottle design - we could try black bottle”. The cup now gives the outlined wordmark a 72 mm wide position above the sailing scene. The bottle explores matte black, a larger ivory Odia wordmark, ivory sails, laterite hull and two restrained straw waves. Sea blue remains a comparison, and black is not a final bottle approval. Both new material renders are illustrative; their geometry and wrap placement are not a production simulation. [Exact refinement prompts](../records/brand-refinements-v3-prompts.json) are retained.

## Both colours, higher-quality construction

The Founder then said “Or we can have both” and “Can we try some high quality water bottle instead”. Both matte black and Nila Sagara blue are retained. The earlier single-wall proposal is superseded by a premium double-wall vacuum-insulated target with SS 304 inner wall, wide-mouth screw cap, carry loop and replaceable seal. The 750 ml size, silicone base detail and precise shape remain proposals; neither colour has been physically sampled. Both colour concepts now share the same refined design.

As a sourcing benchmark, [Borosil Loop 750 ml](https://myborosil.com/products/borosil-loop-vacuum-insulated-bottle-black-750ml) has an indexed manufacturer description listing SS 304, vacuum insulation and powder coating. The direct page redirected to the homepage during verification, so current availability was not established. These are manufacturer claims about that product, not verified performance or a supply agreement for Utkal. Check blank availability, brand/decorating permission, tests, costs and replacement parts before selecting a supplier. No duration claim is transferred to our concept.

[Premium bottle generation prompts](../records/premium-bottle-prompts-v3.json) preserve the two-colour refinement and final concept.

## Tasar Jhula material direction

Founder: “I want Tasar Jhula”. Tasar silk replaces the earlier cotton-canvas outer-shell proposal. Natural golden colour, cotton lining, reinforced handles and the existing English Utkal artwork are proposed; the actual weight, construction and price await supplier samples. Describe outer, lining and handles separately; do not label the whole lined bag “100% silk”. This material change means the bag cannot automatically be treated as the cheapest entry product in the launch budget.

Odisha has a Tasar and silk federation, [SERIFED](https://serifed.odisha.gov.in/), a potential research starting point for fabric provenance. No sourcing relationship, Odisha-made/handloom claim or artisan attribution has been established. Bangalore assembly and Odisha fabric sourcing would be separate facts to verify. [Silk Mark guidance](https://silkmarkindia.com/index.php/faq/) explains that appearance alone cannot establish silk purity; request documented composition and testing as needed. No Silk Mark label is claimed for this concept.

[Exact Tasar mockup prompt](../records/tasar-jhula-prompts-v3.json) is retained. The image illustrates a material direction, not a photograph or certification of a supplied product.
